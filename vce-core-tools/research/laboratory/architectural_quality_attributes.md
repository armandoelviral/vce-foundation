# Architectural Quality Attributes

## Purpose

Architectural Quality Attributes define desirable long-term properties of the VCE Research Program architecture.

Unlike Scientific Objects or Scientific Theories, these attributes describe qualities that the architecture itself should progressively demonstrate.

Their presence must be supported by evidence gathered through Research Cycles.

---

# AQA-001

## Reconstructability

The architecture preserves sufficient information to reconstruct the historical evolution of scientific knowledge.

Status:

Candidate.

---

# AQA-002

## Auditability

Architectural decisions, transitions and scientific evolution remain independently auditable.

Status:

Candidate.

---

# AQA-003

## Evolvability

The architecture is capable of incorporating new theories, domains, scientific instruments and implementations while preserving the stability of its foundational conceptual model.

Status:

Candidate.

---

# AQA-004

## Domain Independence

Foundational theories remain reusable across multiple application domains.

Status:

Candidate.

---

# AQA-005

## Epistemic Traceability

Every Scientific Object preserves reconstructable provenance throughout its lifecycle.

Status:

Candidate.

---

# AQA-006

## Conceptual Decoupling

Conceptual evolution remains independent from operational implementation whenever possible.

Status:

Candidate.

---

# AQA-007

## Theory Independence

Individual theories evolve independently while interacting through explicitly governed interfaces.

Status:

Candidate.

---

# AQA-008

## Architectural Compression

The architecture minimizes the number of foundational concepts without reducing explanatory capability.

Status:

Candidate.

---

# Notes

Architectural Quality Attributes are evaluated continuously.

Promotion from Candidate requires evidence accumulated across multiple Research Cycles.
