# Milestone

The primary objective of the program shifted.

The program no longer organized itself around
software construction.

It became organized around the production,
governance and evolution of reproducible
knowledge.

---

# Before

The principal engineering question was:

"How do we build verifiable software?"

---

# Transition

As Scientific Products emerged,
software became only one component
of a broader architecture.

Attention progressively shifted toward:

- organizational capability;

- institutional memory;

- knowledge governance;

- Human-AI collaboration;

- reproducible laboratories.

---

# After

The central research question became:

"How do organizations produce reproducible knowledge?"

Software remained essential.

It ceased to be the final objective.

It became one instrument
within a larger research program.

---

# Historical Importance

This milestone records the moment at which
the research program adopted knowledge
rather than software as its primary object
of organization.

---

# Consequences

Subsequent research programs are expected
to evaluate software not only by its
technical correctness but also by its
contribution to institutional capability,
scientific evidence and organizational learning.
