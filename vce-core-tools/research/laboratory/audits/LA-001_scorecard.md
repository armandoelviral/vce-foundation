Overall Assessment

The laboratory possesses a highly mature governance and operational architecture.

However, several capabilities required for long-term scientific operation remain underdeveloped, particularly in external validation, reproducibility, ethics and publication.

Overall readiness should therefore be considered **Operational for Internal Research**, but **not yet fully prepared for external scientific collaboration**.
