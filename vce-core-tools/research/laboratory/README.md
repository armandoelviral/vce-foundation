# VCE Research Laboratory

The VCE Research Laboratory is the operational layer of the VCE Research Program.

Its purpose is to execute reproducible research through:

- explicit research cycles
- falsification specifications
- experiments
- evidence acquisition
- evidence cards
- counterexamples
- dependency analysis
- architectural decisions

The laboratory is not a document repository.

It is an operational environment for producing reconstructable scientific evidence.

## Current Status

Operational for internal research.

Not yet fully validated for external scientific collaboration.

## Core Capabilities

- Research governance
- Research operations
- Evidence management
- Experimentation
- Counterexample analysis
- Dependency analysis
- Observatory
- Metrics
- Knowledge integration

## Current Priority

Produce evidence before expanding infrastructure.
