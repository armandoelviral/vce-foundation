# VCE Research Laboratory

The VCE Research Laboratory is the operational layer of the VCE Research Program.

Its purpose is to execute reproducible research through:

- explicit research cycles
- falsification specifications
- experiments
- evidence acquisition
- evidence cards
- counterexamples
- dependency analysis
- architectural decisions

The laboratory is not a document repository.

It is an operational environment for producing reconstructable scientific evidence.

## Current Status

Operational for internal research.

Not yet fully validated for external scientific collaboration.

## Core Capabilities

- Research governance
- Research operations
- Evidence management
- Experimentation
- Counterexample analysis
- Dependency analysis
- Observatory
- Metrics
- Knowledge integration

## Current Priority

Produce evidence before expanding infrastructure.

# Laboratory

The Laboratory research area studies the laboratory itself.

Unlike HAS, which studies reproducible collaboration,
Laboratory research studies reproducible research systems.

Its objective is to understand how laboratories
generate, preserve, govern and evolve knowledge.

The laboratory itself becomes
an object of scientific investigation.

Current research themes include:

- Laboratory History

- Laboratory Governance

- Reproducible Laboratories

- Institutional Memory

- Knowledge Evolution

This research area intentionally remains
independent from any individual Scientific Product.

Its subject of study is the laboratory itself.
