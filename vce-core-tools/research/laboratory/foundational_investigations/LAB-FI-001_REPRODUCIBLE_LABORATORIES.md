# LAB-FI-001 — What Makes a Laboratory Reproducible?

Version

0.1

Status

Future Investigation

---

# Research Question

What makes a laboratory reproducible?

---

# Motivation

Scientific Products appear capable of
institutionalizing organizational capabilities.

Human-AI Standards appears capable of
institutionalizing collaboration.

These developments raise a broader question.

Can an entire research laboratory become
a reproducible system?

---

# Working Questions

Which components of a laboratory are
essential for reproducibility?

Which components are context-dependent?

Can institutional memory itself
be transferred?

Can laboratory culture
be reproduced?

Can governance become portable?

---

# Initial Scope

This investigation intentionally excludes:

• software implementation;

• programming languages;

• infrastructure.

Its focus is the reproducibility
of scientific laboratories.

---

# Initial Research Areas

Investigate:

• laboratory governance;

• institutional memory;

• knowledge maturation;

• Scientific Products;

• Human-AI Standards;

• Scientific Destruction;

• organizational evolution.

---

# Current Evidence

Current evidence consists of a
single laboratory.

No general conclusions are justified.

---

# Success Criteria

The investigation matures only when
multiple independent laboratories
can reproduce comparable behavior
using comparable governance models.
