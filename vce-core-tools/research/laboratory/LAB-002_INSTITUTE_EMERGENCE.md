# LAB-002 — Institute Emergence

Version

1.0

Status

Historical Milestone

---

# Milestone

The research program reached a level of maturity
at which the laboratory naturally expanded into
an institute-level architecture.

This transition was not planned.

It emerged from the accumulation of independent
research programs, governance models,
institutional memory and Scientific Products.

---

# Context

The following disciplines had already emerged:

- Runtime Engineering

- Scientific Product Engineering

- Knowledge Engineering

- Laboratory Engineering

These disciplines no longer represented isolated
projects.

Together they constituted an organized,
self-consistent research ecosystem.

---

# Observable Change

Before

The primary object of study was software.

---

Later

The primary object became Scientific Products.

---

Later

Knowledge systems.

---

Later

Laboratories.

---

Current

The institute itself becomes
an object of scientific investigation.

---

# Consequences

The program now possesses:

• research disciplines;

• governance;

• institutional memory;

• historical milestones;

• reproducible methodology;

• long-term research questions.

These characteristics are consistent with an
emerging research institute.

---

# Historical Importance

This milestone records the moment at which
the architecture ceased to resemble a software
repository and began resembling an organized
research institute.
