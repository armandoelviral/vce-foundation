# Laboratory Gap Register

## Purpose

This document tracks the scientific capabilities that are currently missing from the VCE Research Laboratory.

A gap represents an identifiable limitation affecting the laboratory's ability to produce reconstructable scientific knowledge.

Gaps are managed as research objects rather than informal TODO items.

# Current Assessment

Critical infrastructure gaps have largely been addressed.

Remaining gaps primarily concern scientific validation rather than laboratory construction.

The laboratory should prioritize closing gaps through experiments and evidence rather than additional infrastructure.

---

# Guiding Principle

A gap is closed only when supported by scientific evidence, not by documentation alone.
