# Assessment

The laboratory has reached operational maturity for conducting internally reproducible scientific research.

The next development stage is not additional infrastructure.

The next priority is evidence production through Research Cycles and Scientific Instruments.

---

# Recommendation

Freeze laboratory infrastructure except where justified by experimental evidence.

Allocate future effort to:

- experiments;
- evidence acquisition;
- corpus generation;
- scientific instruments;
- longitudinal validation.
