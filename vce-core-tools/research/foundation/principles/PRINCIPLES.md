# VCE Principles

Version: 1.0

Status: Active

---

## P-001

Evidence Supremacy Principle

Evidence has priority over architecture.

Architecture exists to explain evidence.

---

## P-002

Human Expertise Amplification Principle

Scientific Products preserve, amplify and institutionalize human expertise.

---

## P-003

Capability over Automation Principle

Automation is a mechanism.

Institutional Capability is the objective.

---

## P-004

Institutional Capability Principle

Organizations become permanently stronger by transforming validated expertise into governed institutional capability.

---

## P-005

Research Persistence Principle

Validated discoveries shall become permanent repository artifacts before continuing research.

---

## P-006

Governance Before Promotion Principle

No knowledge becomes permanent institutional capability without governed validation.

---

## P-007

Institutional Learning Principle

Organizations learn through governed integration of evidence, expertise and validated reasoning.
