# H-007

Title

Capability-Driven Platform Hypothesis

Status

Candidate

---

## Hypothesis

Future long-lived software platforms will increasingly be designed to create, preserve and evolve institutional capabilities rather than merely automate operational tasks.

These platforms will integrate:

- Human Expertise
- Specifications
- Software
- Operational Evidence
- Governance
- Institutional Memory

into a continuous capability evolution cycle.

---

## Research Question

Does explicit management of Institutional Capability produce more sustainable organizational evolution than traditional software-centric platforms?

---

## Validation Strategy

Compare:

Traditional software lifecycle

vs

Capability-driven lifecycle

using SP001 as the first reference implementation.

---

## Possible Falsification

The capability-driven architecture introduces excessive complexity without measurable gains in:

- organizational learning;
- decision quality;
- knowledge reuse;
- adaptability;
- long-term maintainability.
