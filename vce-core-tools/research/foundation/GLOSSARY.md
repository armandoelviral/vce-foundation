# VCE Glossary

Version: 1.0

Status: Active

---

## Scientific Object

A governed knowledge artifact with explicit identity, lifecycle and evidence.

---

## Scientific Product

An operational system designed to:

- generate operational value;
- generate governed evidence;
- preserve human expertise;
- increase institutional capability;
- improve the Research Program.

---

## Program Capability

A permanent capability acquired by the Research Program through validated research and governed institutionalization.

---

## Institutional Capability

A permanent organizational capability created through the integration of human expertise, operational evidence, institutional knowledge, artificial intelligence and governance.

---

## Institutional Learning

The governed transformation of validated expertise and evidence into reusable organizational capability.

---

## Institutional Memory

Governed repository preserving validated organizational reasoning, decisions and operational evidence.

---

## Human Expertise

Contextual judgment acquired through experience and professional practice.

Scientific Products preserve and amplify Human Expertise.

---

## Operational Evidence

Evidence generated through real-world operation.

---

## Capability Growth

Measured increase of permanent organizational capability over time.

---

## Governance

Mechanisms responsible for validating, promoting and preserving institutional knowledge.

---

## Program Milestone

Permanent historical record documenting an irreversible increase in the capabilities of the Research Program.

---

## Methodology Baseline

Official versioned research methodology governing future Research Cycles.

---

## Research Freeze

Governed snapshot preserving the epistemological state of a Research Cycle.

---

## Legacy

Permanent knowledge promoted beyond the lifetime of an individual Research Cycle.
