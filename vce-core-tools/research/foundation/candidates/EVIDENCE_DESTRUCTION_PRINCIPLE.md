# Evidence Destruction Principle

Status

Candidate

---

Hypothesis

A Scientific Product shall not preserve architectural assumptions, methodologies or organizational capabilities solely because they already exist.

Elements that fail to survive governed evidence shall be revised, restricted or retired.

---

Principle

The objective of evidence is not only to validate.

Its objective is also to eliminate.

---

Corollary

Institutional Capability grows not only by acquiring better knowledge.

It also grows by removing obsolete knowledge.
