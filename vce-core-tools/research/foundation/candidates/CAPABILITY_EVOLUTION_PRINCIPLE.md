# Capability Evolution Principle

Status

Candidate

---

Hypothesis

Institutional Capability should evolve through governed introduction, refinement and retirement of capabilities.

Retirement is not failure.

Retirement preserves the organization's capacity to adapt.

---

Principle

The purpose of governed retirement is not destruction.

Its purpose is to create space for superior organizational capability.

---

Corollary

Every retired capability contributes historical evidence.

Historical evidence remains available for audit, replay and learning.

Retired capabilities shall never become invisible.

They become historical knowledge.
