# Experiment Protocol

Every experiment shall explicitly define:

---

## Experiment Identifier

EXP-XXXX

---

## Related Research Cycle

RC

---

## Research Question

RQ

---

## Related Hypothesis

DH or H

---

## Objective

What is being evaluated?

---

## Falsification Criterion

What result would invalidate the hypothesis?

---

## Methodology

How will the experiment be performed?

---

## Inputs

Required datasets, literature or implementations.

---

## Procedure

Step-by-step execution.

---

## Expected Results

Before execution.

---

## Observations

Recorded during execution.

---

## Evidence Produced

Experimental

Comparative

Operational

Formal

---

## Limitations

Known constraints.

---

## Conclusion

Supported

Partially Supported

Rejected

Inconclusive

---

## Next Action

What should happen after this experiment?
