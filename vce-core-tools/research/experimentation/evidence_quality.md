# Evidence Quality Assessment

Evidence quality shall be evaluated independently from experimental outcome.

Evaluation Criteria

---

## Reproducibility

Can another researcher repeat the experiment?

---

## Traceability

Can every step be reconstructed?

---

## Transparency

Are assumptions explicitly documented?

---

## Methodological Discipline

Was the predefined protocol followed?

---

## Falsifiability

Could the experiment genuinely contradict the hypothesis?

---

## Evidence Classification

Low

Moderate

High

Very High

The objective is not to maximize positive results.

The objective is to maximize confidence in the scientific process.
