# Evidence Cards

## Purpose

Evidence Cards are the canonical representation of external scientific evidence within the VCE Research Program.

Their objective is not to summarize publications.

Their objective is to normalize heterogeneous scientific knowledge into a common structure that can be systematically compared, audited and related to the research program.

Every external source shall first become an Evidence Card before influencing any Design Hypothesis, Scientific Hypothesis, Model, Accepted Principle or Constitutional Knowledge.

---

# Why Evidence Cards?

Scientific literature originates from multiple disciplines.

Each discipline describes evidence differently.

Examples include:

- journal articles
- conference papers
- standards
- RFCs
- books
- technical reports
- doctoral dissertations
- reference implementations

Without normalization, meaningful comparison becomes increasingly difficult.

Evidence Cards provide a common representation independent of source format.

---

# Objectives

Evidence Cards support:

- literature comparison
- hypothesis evaluation
- novelty assessment
- contradiction analysis
- provenance reconstruction
- Knowledge Graph integration
- Research Metrics
- future automation

---

# Guiding Principle

The VCE Research Program does not compare documents.

It compares normalized scientific evidence.

Evidence Cards are therefore considered the canonical observational unit for external scientific knowledge.

# Scientific Role

Evidence Cards do not establish truth.

They document observations extracted from scientific sources.

Interpretation occurs only during Comparative Evidence synthesis.

# Evidence Categories

Each Evidence Card may contribute one or more evidence types.

- Comparative Evidence (CE)
- Formal Evidence (FE)
- Experimental Evidence (EE)
- Operational Evidence (OE)

The assigned category reflects how the evidence contributes to the research program rather than the publication type.

---

# Research Discipline

Evidence Cards are intentionally descriptive.

They do not claim novelty.

They do not interpret results beyond what the source supports.

They preserve sufficient information for future researchers to independently reconstruct the comparison process.

---

# Long-Term Vision

Over time, the collection of Evidence Cards becomes a structured scientific memory of the VCE Research Program.

Rather than relying on isolated references, future researchers will be able to reconstruct:

- which literature was considered;
- how it was interpreted;
- which hypotheses it supported;
- which hypotheses it contradicted;
- how those conclusions evolved over time.

The objective is not merely to preserve references.

It is to preserve the evolution of scientific understanding.
