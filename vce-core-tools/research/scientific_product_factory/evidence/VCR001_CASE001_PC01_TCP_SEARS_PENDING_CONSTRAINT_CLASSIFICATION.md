## Interpretation Rules

DIRECTLY_OBSERVABLE

means that an applicable

human observation

may be possible

from the available

external evidence.

DERIVED

means that the result

depends on other
declared observations

or an explicit
profile boundary.

A derived result

shall not be counted

as an independent
commercial improvement.

INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE

means that

required calibration,

measurement,

tolerance,

or comparable evidence

has not been established.

CONTEXT_DISPUTED

means that

the applicable context

is affected by

an explicit

source discrepancy.

A classification

shall not substitute

for an actual
evaluation decision.
