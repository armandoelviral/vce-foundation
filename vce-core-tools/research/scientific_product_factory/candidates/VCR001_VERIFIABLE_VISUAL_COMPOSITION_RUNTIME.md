Investigate whether

visual composition

can become

a deterministic,

evidence-producing,

replayable,

shall remain

inside the
Retail Vertical Pack.

shall not modify

VCE Core,

HAS Core,

or foundational
constitutional semantics.

whether approved
domain knowledge

can be transformed

into verifiable
visual compositions.

is

Retail VM Composer.

## Fixture Slot Candidate

A Fixture Slot

represents

a governed
visual placement capacity

within a fixture.

A Fixture Slot

is not merely

a coordinate.

It may constrain:

supported object type,

position,

orientation,

dimensions,

capacity,

visibility,

lighting,

occlusion,

mounting,

adjacency,

and applicability.

---

## Geometry Dimension

Geometry may include:

position,

orientation,

width,

height,

depth,

support plane,

placement volume,

collision boundary,

dimensional tolerance,

adjacency,

and camera-relative
projection.

Geometry shall answer:

Can the object

be placed

without violating

physical or compositional
constraints?

---

## Photometry Dimension

Photometry may include:

light direction,

light intensity,

color temperature,

uniformity,

contrast,

shadow,

reflectance,

and calibration confidence.

Photometry shall answer:

Can the object

be perceived

under the declared

lighting context?

---

## Foreground Occlusion Candidate

Foreground Occlusion

is an unvalidated
candidate dimension.

It may include:

visible-area ratio,

foreground overlap,

fixture obstruction,

product obstruction,

signage obstruction,

camera line of sight,

viewpoint,

and semantic
visibility threshold.

Foreground Occlusion

shall answer:

Does the target

remain sufficiently
visible

from the declared
observation context?

No fixed threshold

shall be invented

without empirical
Retail evidence.

---

## Falsification Conditions

This candidate

shall be weakened,

reduced,

or refuted

if evidence demonstrates:

users do not value
reproducibility;

calibration costs exceed
commercial value;

fixture profiles cannot
be reused;

geometry cannot be
reliably reconstructed;

photometric evidence
does not affect decisions;

occlusion cannot be
measured usefully;

expert corrections cannot
be represented;

or the workflow

does not reduce

time,

cost,

errors,

or execution variance.
