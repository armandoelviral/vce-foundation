# Retail Product Model Hypotheses

Version: 0.1

Status: Candidate

Classification: Product and Business Hypotheses

---

## H-RPM-001

A modular suite composed of VM Composer, Fixture Calibrator and Cloud Assets provides greater user value than a single monolithic Retail application.

Falsification:

Users consistently prefer a unified workflow and show low adoption of specialized applications.

---

## H-RPM-002

A self-service subscription model can acquire individual VM professionals and small teams without enterprise sales intervention.

Falsification:

Customer acquisition cost, onboarding complexity or trust requirements make self-service conversion economically unsustainable.

---

## H-RPM-003

A freemium export with watermark increases qualified conversion to paid plans.

Falsification:

Watermarked usage generates operating cost without meaningful paid conversion or reduces perceived professional value.

---

## H-RPM-004

A marketplace of governed, calibrated fixture profiles creates reusable economic value.

Falsification:

Demand is insufficient, calibration quality varies excessively, or intellectual-property restrictions prevent scalable distribution.

---

## H-RPM-005

CPU-based geometric and photometric processing can support the initial workload at materially lower cost than diffusion-based generative rendering.

Falsification:

Quality requirements, concurrent demand or complex scenes require GPU-intensive processing.

---

## H-RPM-006

Portable and versioned project packages increase workflow continuity between professionals, agencies and retailers.

Falsification:

Users do not transfer projects, or incompatible assets and policies prevent practical reproducibility.

---

## H-RPM-007

The initial proposed pricing tiers produce acceptable willingness-to-pay and sustainable unit economics.

Falsification:

Pilot users reject pricing, support costs exceed contribution margin, or storage and transfer costs invalidate the model.

---

## Evidence Required

- user interviews;
- prototype usage;
- conversion tests;
- infrastructure telemetry;
- support effort;
- storage and transfer cost;
- project portability tests;
- marketplace legal review;
- longitudinal retention.
