# Capability Operating System

Status

Candidate

---

## Hypothesis

Scientific Products may require an orchestration layer responsible for managing the continuous evolution of Institutional Capabilities.

The Capability Operating System does not execute business logic.

It governs capability evolution.

---

## Responsibilities

Observe platform events.

Track Capability Lifecycles.

Measure Institutional Capability Growth.

Trigger governance workflows.

Schedule longitudinal reviews.

Detect capability drift.

Coordinate capability evolution.

---

## Does NOT own

Retail logic.

Healthcare logic.

Simulation.

Recommendations.

Rendering.

Assets.

---

## Produces

Capability evolution workflows.

Governance requests.

Monitoring plans.

Capability health reports.
