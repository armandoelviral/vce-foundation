# Scientific Product Genome

Version: 0.1

Status: Candidate

Classification: Foundation / Evolution

---

# Purpose

Define the evolutionary rules governing every Scientific Product developed under the VCE Research Program.

The Genome specifies what may evolve, what must remain stable and how Scientific Products preserve identity while continuously improving through evidence.

The Genome governs evolution.

The DNA governs identity.

---

# Evolution Philosophy

Scientific Products are expected to evolve.

Evolution is not optional.

Uncontrolled evolution is prohibited.

Every evolutionary change shall preserve:

- identity;
- governance;
- reconstructibility;
- institutional capability.
