# Scientific Product DNA

Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

Classification: Foundation

---

# Purpose

Define the irreducible architecture shared by every Scientific Product developed under the VCE Research Program.

Every Scientific Product shall preserve this DNA regardless of industry or operational domain.

---

# DNA Components

Every Scientific Product shall contain the following permanent components.

---

## Human Expertise Layer

Captures professional judgment.

Transforms expert reasoning into institutional knowledge.

---

## Artificial Intelligence Layer

Produces analysis, simulation and recommendations.

Artificial Intelligence augments reasoning.

It never defines permanent organizational capability by itself.

---

## Evidence Layer

Collects operational outcomes.

Measures recommendation quality.

Produces reproducible evidence.

---

## Governance Layer

Validates candidate knowledge.

Promotes validated knowledge.

Protects institutional integrity.

---

## Institutional Memory

Preserves:

- reasoning;
- decisions;
- context;
- evidence;
- outcomes.

Institutional Memory is cumulative.

---

## Learning Engine

Transforms:

Operational Evidence

+

Human Expertise

↓

Institutional Learning

↓

Candidate Capability

---

## Capability Layer

Produces permanent institutional capabilities.

Represents the primary outcome of the Scientific Product.

---

## Feedback Layer

Continuously compares:

Expected Outcome

↓

Observed Outcome

↓

Capability Evolution

---

# DNA Rule

Removing any DNA component changes the nature of the Scientific Product.

The product may continue functioning operationally.

It shall no longer qualify as a Scientific Product under the VCE Research Program.

---

# Closing Statement

Scientific Products are operational systems designed to transform evidence and expertise into governed institutional capabilities.

The DNA preserves that purpose across every future implementation.
