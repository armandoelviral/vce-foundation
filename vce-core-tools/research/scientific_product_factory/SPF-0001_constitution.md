# Scientific Product Factory

## Constitution

Version

0.1

Status

Draft

---

# Mission

Create governed Scientific Products capable of increasing permanent organizational capabilities through the integration of:

- Human Expertise
- Operational Evidence
- Artificial Intelligence
- Institutional Governance

---

# Scientific Product Definition

A Scientific Product is an operational system designed not only to generate value, but also to generate validated evidence capable of improving the Research Program itself.

---

# Primary Objective

Increase Institutional Capability.

Not automation.

Not replacement.

Capability.

---

# Secondary Objectives

Generate evidence.

Capture expertise.

Preserve organizational knowledge.

Institutionalize successful reasoning.

Continuously improve organizational capabilities.

---

# Guiding Principle

Artificial Intelligence augments analysis.

Human Expertise provides judgment.

Institutional Governance determines permanence.

---

# Success Metric

Growth of Institutional Capability.

---

# Failure Metric

Replacement of expertise without growth of organizational capability.

---

# Long-Term Vision

Organizations shall become progressively more capable without depending upon irreversible loss of human expertise.
