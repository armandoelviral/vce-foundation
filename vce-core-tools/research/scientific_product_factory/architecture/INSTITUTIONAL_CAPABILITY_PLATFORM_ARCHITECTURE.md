# Institutional Capability Platform

Version: 0.1

Status: Draft

Classification: Architecture

---

# Purpose

Provide the reference architecture capable of producing Institutional Capabilities through Scientific Products.

The platform shall be domain-independent.

Verticals become configurations rather than architectures.

---

# Architectural Layers

## Layer 1

Human Expertise

Captures:

- reasoning;
- contextual knowledge;
- strategic intent;
- validated experience.

---

## Layer 2

Artificial Intelligence

Provides:

- recommendations;
- simulations;
- predictions;
- optimization.

Artificial Intelligence augments analysis.

---

## Layer 3

Operational Evidence

Captures:

- execution;
- KPIs;
- outcomes;
- compliance;
- business impact.

---

## Layer 4

Institutional Memory

Preserves:

- decisions;
- reasoning;
- evidence;
- policies;
- history.

---

## Layer 5

Governance

Determines:

- validation;
- promotion;
- policy approval;
- capability creation.

---

## Layer 6

Institutional Capability

Produces permanent organizational capabilities.

This layer represents the primary output of the platform.

---

# Design Objective

Increase Institutional Capability continuously.

Not simply improve recommendation quality.

---

# Platform Nature

The platform is domain-independent.

Retail

Healthcare

Manufacturing

Hospitality

Education

Logistics

become Vertical Packs operating over the same capability platform.

---

# Closing Statement

The Institutional Capability Platform exists to transform expertise and operational evidence into governed organizational capability.

Scientific Products are operational realizations of this architecture.
