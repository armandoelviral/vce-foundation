# CFS-007 — We Do Not Cultivate Ideas. We Cultivate the Conditions Under Which the Best Ideas Survive.

Status

Candidate Foundational Statement

---

## Statement

We do not cultivate ideas.

We cultivate the conditions
under which the best ideas survive.

---

## Motivation

Throughout the Scientific Product Program,
the laboratory consistently avoided protecting
individual ideas from criticism.

Instead, effort was invested in building an
environment where ideas could be:

- implemented;
- challenged;
- compared;
- simplified;
- rejected;
- improved;
- institutionalized.

The objective was never to maximize
the number of ideas.

The objective was to maximize the quality
of the ideas that survived.

---

## Interpretation

The value of a laboratory is not determined
by how many ideas it generates.

Its value is determined by the quality of the
selection process that determines which ideas remain.

The laboratory therefore invests primarily in:

- evidence;
- reproducibility;
- Scientific Destruction;
- institutional memory;
- independent validation.

Ideas emerge naturally from those conditions.

---

## Expected Consequences

If this statement survives repeated application:

- fewer weak ideas survive;
- stronger theories emerge;
- scientific debt decreases;
- institutional learning accelerates.

---

## Current Evidence

Observed during:

- VCE
- SP001
- HAS
- Research Program
- Institutionalization Sprint

Evidence remains insufficient.

Independent validation required.

---

Status
