# CFS-001 — Evidence Precedes Opinion

Status

Candidate Foundational Statement

---

## Statement

Evidence precedes opinion.

---

## Motivation

Throughout the evolution of the Scientific Product
Program, architectural decisions that survived
were consistently supported by implementation
and reproducible evidence.

Opinions frequently initiated exploration.

Evidence determined what remained.

---

## Interpretation

Opinions are valuable as generators of hypotheses.

Evidence determines whether those hypotheses
should continue evolving.

The laboratory therefore distinguishes between:

Opinion

↓

Hypothesis

↓

Implementation

↓

Evidence

↓

Scientific Destruction

↓

Candidate Principle

↓

Independent Validation

↓

Principle

---

## Expected Consequences

If this statement survives repeated application:

- decisions become evidence-driven;
- architectural drift decreases;
- discussions become reproducible;
- institutional memory improves.
