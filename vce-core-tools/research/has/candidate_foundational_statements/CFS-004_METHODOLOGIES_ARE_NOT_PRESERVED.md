# CFS-004 — Methodologies Are Not Preserved. They Evolve.

Status

Candidate Foundational Statement

---

## Statement

Methodologies are not preserved.

They evolve.

---

## Motivation

Throughout the evolution of the Scientific Product
Program, the methodology itself repeatedly changed.

Each implementation generated observations that
modified the laboratory workflow.

The laboratory did not protect its methodology
from change.

It subjected the methodology itself to the same
cycle of observation, implementation,
Scientific Destruction and evidence
used for software.

---

## Interpretation

A methodology is not a static artifact.

It is a Scientific Product.

Like any other Scientific Product,
its purpose is not permanence.

Its purpose is continuous improvement
through evidence.

---

## Evolution Cycle

Observation

↓

Implementation

↓

Evidence

↓

Scientific Destruction

↓

Methodological Improvement

↓

Institutionalization

↓

Next Generation

---

## Expected Consequences

If this statement survives repeated application:

- methodologies become adaptive;
- learning accelerates;
- institutional memory improves;
- scientific rigor increases;
- methodological debt decreases.

---

## Current Evidence

Observed during:

- VCE
- SP001
- HAS
- Research Program

Multiple workflow refinements were
introduced after implementation rather
than before it.

Further validation required.

---

Status

Promotion requires:

- repeated methodological evolution;
- evidence across multiple Scientific Products;
- successful Scientific Destruction;
- independent validation.
