# CFS-008 — A Self-Correcting Laboratory

Status

Candidate Foundational Statement

---

## Statement

Our goal is not to always be right.

Our goal is to build a laboratory capable
of correcting itself with every generation.

---

## Motivation

No scientific program can guarantee that every
hypothesis, abstraction or methodology is correct.

What can be designed is something more robust:

a laboratory that continuously improves its own
understanding through observation, evidence,
Scientific Destruction and institutional learning.

The objective therefore shifts from certainty
to continuous correction.

---

## Interpretation

The laboratory is itself an evolving system.

Its success should not be measured by the number
of correct decisions made in the past.

It should be measured by its demonstrated ability
to detect, understand and correct its own errors.

Continuous correction is treated as a capability,
not as a failure.

---

## Expected Consequences

If this statement survives repeated application:

- methodological resilience increases;
- scientific debt decreases;
- institutional learning accelerates;
- long-term knowledge quality improves.

---

## Current Evidence

Observed during:

- VCE evolution;
- Scientific Product Runtime;
- HAS evolution;
- Research Program;
- Institutionalization Sprint.

Evidence remains insufficient.

Independent validation required.
