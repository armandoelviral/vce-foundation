# CFS-002 — Survival Precedes Promotion

Status

Candidate Foundational Statement

---

## Statement

Survival precedes promotion.

---

## Motivation

Throughout the Scientific Product Program,
architectural ideas were never promoted because
they appeared elegant or intuitive.

Promotion occurred only after repeated
implementation, evidence accumulation and
successful resistance to Scientific Destruction.

The laboratory therefore adopted a simple rule:

No idea advances solely because it is compelling.

It advances because it survives.

---

## Interpretation

Every candidate idea follows the same
evolutionary pathway.

Observation

↓

Hypothesis

↓

Implementation

↓

Evidence

↓

Scientific Destruction

↓

Candidate Principle

↓

Independent Validation

↓

Principle

Promotion is therefore an emergent consequence
of survival rather than a design decision.

---

## Expected Consequences

If this statement survives repeated application:

- premature standardization decreases;
- architectural stability increases;
- scientific rigor improves;
- principles emerge naturally.

---

## Current Evidence

- Transition Pattern

Further validation required.

---

## Promotion Criteria

Promotion requires:

- successful implementation in multiple contexts;
- repeated survival after Scientific Destruction;
- independent validation;
- reproducible evidence across Scientific Products.

---

## Laboratory Note

The laboratory summarized this statement as:

> Principles are not promoted.
> They survive until promotion becomes inevitable.
