# CFS-003 — Principles Are Not Designed. Principles Survive.

Status

Candidate Foundational Statement

---

## Statement

Principles are not designed.

Principles survive.

---

## Motivation

Throughout the evolution of the Scientific Product
Program, no principle was intentionally created.

Instead, recurring patterns repeatedly emerged
from independent implementations.

Only after surviving implementation,
evidence accumulation,
Scientific Destruction,
and continued reuse,
did those patterns begin exhibiting
the characteristics expected of principles.

---

## Interpretation

A principle is not the result of intention.

A principle is the consequence of survival.

The laboratory therefore refuses to promote
ideas based on elegance, intuition or preference.

Only repeated survival grants a statement
the opportunity to become a principle.

## Expected Consequences

If this statement survives repeated application:

- premature principles disappear;
- architectural stability increases;
- methodological rigor improves;
- principles become observable phenomena
  rather than design decisions.
