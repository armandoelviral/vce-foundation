# FI-005 — When Does Observation Become Theory?

Status

Foundational Investigation

---

## Research Question

When does observation become theory?

---

## Motivation

Throughout the Scientific Product Program,
individual observations repeatedly accumulated.

Some remained isolated.

Others began exhibiting stable regularities across
independent implementations.

Eventually those regularities appeared capable of
explaining previously unrelated phenomena.

This transition motivated the present investigation.

---

## Working Questions

How many independent observations are required
before theory becomes scientifically justified?

Is recurrence sufficient?

Is prediction required?

Can explanatory power be measured?

Can a theory emerge without intentional design?

---

## Initial Observations

Current evidence suggests that theory does not
appear immediately after observation.

Instead, an intermediate evolutionary process
seems to exist.

Observation

↓

Recurrence

↓

Abstraction

↓

Explanatory Structure

↓

Theory

Each stage appears to require stronger evidence
than the previous one.

---

## Research Objectives

Investigate:

• relationship between recurrence and abstraction;

• minimum evidence required for theory formation;

• predictive capability;

• falsifiability;

• independent validation;

• relationship with Scientific Products.

---

## Success Criteria

The investigation will be considered mature
when:

- objective criteria distinguish abstraction
  from theory;

- explanatory power becomes measurable;

- predictions derived from the theory can be
  experimentally evaluated.

## Laboratory Note

One recurring expression summarized the
motivation for this investigation:

> Theory emerges when sufficiently diverse
> implementations begin to rhyme with one another.

The statement remains metaphorical.

The objective of this investigation is to
determine whether that intuition can be
expressed as a reproducible scientific model.
