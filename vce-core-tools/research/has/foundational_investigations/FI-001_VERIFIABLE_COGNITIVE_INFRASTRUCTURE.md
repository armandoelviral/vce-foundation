# FI-001 — What Is Verifiable Cognitive Infrastructure?

Status

Foundational Investigation

---

## Research Question

What is Verifiable Cognitive Infrastructure?

---

## Motivation

The Scientific Product Program repeatedly
produced systems whose objective extended
beyond software.

The recurring objective appeared to be the
construction of an infrastructure capable of
creating, validating, preserving and evolving
organizational knowledge through reproducible
evidence.

This recurring pattern motivated the present
investigation.

---

## Working Question

Under what conditions does a collection of
Scientific Products become a Verifiable
Cognitive Infrastructure?

---

## Current Working Definition

A Verifiable Cognitive Infrastructure is an
evolving system capable of institutionalizing
organizational capabilities through evidence,
reproducibility and continuous validation.

This definition is provisional.

Its validity depends on future evidence.

---

## Research Objectives

Investigate:

• necessary architectural properties;

• necessary methodological properties;

• relationship with Scientific Products;

• relationship with Human-AI Standards;

• mechanisms for institutional learning;

• mechanisms for organizational memory.

---

## Success Criteria

This investigation will be considered mature
only when:

- multiple independent Scientific Products
  satisfy the proposed definition;

- architectural invariants become identifiable;

- predictions derived from the definition
  are experimentally validated.
