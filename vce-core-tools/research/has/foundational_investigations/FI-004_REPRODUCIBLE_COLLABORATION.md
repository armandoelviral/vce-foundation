# FI-004 — What Makes Collaboration Reproducible?

Status

Foundational Investigation

---

## Research Question

What makes collaboration reproducible?

---

## Motivation

Scientific Products require reproducibility.

However, throughout the Scientific Product Program,
an additional question emerged.

Can collaboration itself become reproducible?

If collaboration can repeatedly generate:

- architectural improvements;
- methodological improvements;
- institutional knowledge;
- Scientific Products;

then reproducibility may depend less on the
participants themselves and more on the protocol
that governs their interaction.

This motivates the present investigation.

---

## Working Questions

Which properties of collaboration
are reproducible?

Which properties remain inherently individual?

Can collaboration protocols become
institutional capabilities?

Can Human-AI collaboration become
experimentally repeatable?

---

## Initial Observations

Current evidence suggests that collaboration
is influenced by at least four interacting factors.

• shared objectives;

• explicit institutional memory;

• evidence-driven decision making;

• continuous methodological evolution.

The relative importance of these factors
remains unknown.

---

## Research Objectives

Investigate:

• reproducibility of collaboration protocols;

• role of HAS;

• role of institutional memory;

• role of Scientific Destruction;

• role of governance;

• mechanisms that improve collaboration
  independently of specific participants.

---

## Success Criteria

The investigation will be considered mature
when:

- collaboration protocols can be reproduced
  across independent teams;

- measurable indicators of collaboration quality
  are defined;

- HAS demonstrates predictive value across
  multiple Scientific Product Programs.
