# H-CI-002 — Institutionalized Capabilities

Status

Hypothesis

---

## Hypothesis

The primary purpose of a Scientific Product
is to institutionalize an organizational capability
through reproducible evidence.

---

## Motivation

During the evolution of VCE,
Scientific Products were initially viewed
as software systems.

As additional implementations emerged,
a different pattern became visible.

The distinguishing characteristic was not
the technology employed.

The distinguishing characteristic was the
organizational capability that each product
institutionalized.

---

## Working Definition

A Scientific Product is a system whose
primary purpose is to institutionalize
a capability through reproducible evidence.

Software is one possible implementation.

Institutionalization is the defining property.

---

## Expected Properties

If the hypothesis is correct,
every Scientific Product should answer
the following question:

"What organizational capability
does this product institutionalize?"

Products unable to answer this question
may not qualify as Scientific Products.

---

## Experimental Predictions

Different Scientific Products should
institutionalize different capabilities
while sharing a common architectural model.

Examples include:

- trust;
- learning;
- collaboration;
- decision quality;
- organizational memory.

---

## Current Evidence

Observed during:

- VCE
- SP001
- HAS

Evidence remains insufficient.

Independent validation required.
