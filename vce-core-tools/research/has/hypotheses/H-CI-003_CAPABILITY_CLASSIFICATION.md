# H-CI-003 — Capability Classification

Status

Hypothesis

---

## Hypothesis

Scientific Products can be classified according
to the organizational capability they
institutionalize rather than the technology
used to implement them.

---

## Motivation

Traditional software classifications are based on:

- programming language;
- architecture;
- deployment model;
- business domain.

During the Scientific Product Program,
a different organizational pattern emerged.

Products naturally grouped themselves by the
capability they institutionalized.

---

## Working Definition

The defining characteristic of a Scientific Product
is not its implementation technology.

It is the capability that becomes
institutionalized through reproducible evidence.

---

---

Future Scientific Products may institutionalize:

- Clinical Knowledge
- Manufacturing Optimization
- Decision Quality
- Organizational Memory
- Supply Chain Assurance
- Risk Evaluation

without requiring changes to the
Scientific Product architecture.

---

## Expected Properties

If the hypothesis is correct:

- new Scientific Products should be created
  by defining the capability to institutionalize;

- architectural reuse should remain high;

- organizational diversity should increase
  without increasing conceptual complexity.

---

## Experimental Predictions

Independent Scientific Products created in
different domains should still conform
to the same Scientific Product Runtime.

The capability changes.

The architecture remains stable.

---

## Current Evidence

- VCE
- SP001
- HAS

Evidence remains insufficient.

Independent validation required.
