# H-HAS-001 — Collaboration as a Scientific Product

Status

Hypothesis

---

## Hypothesis

Human-AI collaboration itself can be modeled
as a Scientific Product.

---

## Motivation

During the evolution of VCE,
Scientific Products,
Transition Pattern,
RuntimeResult,
Scientific Destruction,
and HAS,
the collaboration protocol itself began evolving.

The collaboration generated:

- architectural improvements;
- methodological improvements;
- new research questions;
- new Scientific Products;
- improvements to the collaboration protocol itself.

This suggests that collaboration may itself
constitute a Scientific Product.

---

## Expected Properties

If the hypothesis is correct,
Human-AI collaboration should exhibit:

- reproducibility;
- institutional memory;
- measurable evolution;
- capability growth;
- scientific destruction;
- evidence accumulation.

---

## Experimental Predictions

The collaboration should become:

- observable;
- measurable;
- reproducible;
- improvable.

Independent collaborations should be able
to validate or refute the model.

---

## Current Evidence

Observed during:

- VCE
- Scientific Product Runtime
- Runtime simplification
- RuntimeResult
- Transition Pattern
- HAS emergence

Evidence remains insufficient.

Independent validation required.

---

Status

Hypothesis

Not promoted to Candidate Principle.
