# Purpose

Human-AI Standards (HAS) defines the governance
model through which observations evolve into
institutional knowledge.

The objective of this document is not to govern
software.

Its objective is to govern the maturation of
knowledge.

---

# Core Principle

Knowledge is not promoted.

Knowledge matures.

Every promotion requires evidence.

Every promotion remains reversible.

---

# Knowledge Evolution Pipeline

Research Question

↓

Observation

↓

Hypothesis

↓

Candidate Foundational Statement

↓

Candidate Principle

↓

Principle

↓

Institutional Standard

Each transition represents an increase in
evidence rather than authority.

---

# Promotion Rule

No artifact advances because of preference,
seniority or consensus alone.

Promotion requires evidence appropriate to
its maturity level.

---

# Demotion Rule

Institutional knowledge remains falsifiable.

If contradictory evidence emerges,
any promoted artifact may return to a
previous maturity level.

Knowledge governance therefore supports
both promotion and demotion.

---

# Scientific Destruction

Scientific Destruction is the primary
validation mechanism before promotion.

Every candidate must survive deliberate
attempts at refutation.

Failure is treated as evidence.

Not as error.

---

# Institutional Memory

Every promotion produces:

• traceability;

• rationale;

• evidence references;

• implementation history;

• review history.

Institutional memory becomes cumulative.

---

# Governance Objective

The laboratory does not attempt to protect
its conclusions.

It protects the integrity of the process
through which conclusions are reached.
