# OBS-0003 — Abstraction and Theory

Status

Observation

---

## Statement

Abstraction is to software
what theory is to science.

Both emerge when multiple independent implementations
reveal a common underlying structure.

---

## Context

During the evolution of Scientific Products,
multiple software implementations began exhibiting
the same architectural pattern.

The engineering response was abstraction.

From the perspective of scientific methodology,
the equivalent process is theory formation.

---

## Observation

Software engineering and scientific reasoning
share the same structural mechanism.

Engineering compresses repeated implementations
into abstractions.

Science compresses repeated observations
into theories.

---

## Interpretation

The emergence of an abstraction and the emergence
of a scientific theory are manifestations of the
same cognitive process:

identifying stable regularities across
independent instances.

---

## Consequence

Well-designed software architectures and mature
scientific theories both arise from repeated,
independent evidence rather than isolated ideas.
