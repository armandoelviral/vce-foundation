# OBS-0002 — Theory Emergence

Status

Observation

---

## Statement

After multiple independent implementations,
we observe a set of structural regularities that,
to the extent supported by current evidence,
appear to explain the common behavior
of these systems.

---

## Context

The theory was not proposed before implementation.

Instead, repeated implementations began exhibiting
the same architectural patterns.

Only after sufficient recurrence did an explanatory
structure begin to emerge.

---

## Observation

Theory was not imposed.

Theory emerged.

---

## Scientific Interpretation

A theory begins to emerge when multiple independent
implementations exhibit structural regularities that
resist deliberate attempts at refutation.

---

## Consequence

Implementation precedes explanation.

Repeated evidence precedes theory.

---

## Current Evidence

- HAS emergence

Further validation required.
