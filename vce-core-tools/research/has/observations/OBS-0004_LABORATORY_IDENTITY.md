# OBS-0004 — Laboratory Identity

Status

Observation

---

## Statement

We cultivate a laboratory that learns,
prunes,
grows again,
and allows evidence to decide
what deserves to remain.

---

## Context

During the evolution of the Scientific Product Program,
the laboratory repeatedly demonstrated a recurring cycle.

Ideas were not accumulated indefinitely.

Instead they were:

- implemented,
- challenged,
- simplified,
- pruned,
- reconstructed,
- and preserved only when supported by evidence.

This recurring behavior became an observable property
of the laboratory itself.

---

## Observation

The laboratory behaves as an evolutionary system.

Growth is only one phase.

Pruning is equally necessary.

Without pruning,
complexity accumulates.

Without growth,
learning stops.

Evidence determines which ideas survive.

## Implication

The objective of the laboratory is not to maximize
the number of ideas.

Its objective is to maximize the quality
of the ideas that survive.
