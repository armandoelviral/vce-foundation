# OBS-0010 — Institutional Memory Threshold

Status

Observation

---

## Statement

There exists a threshold at which research ceases to depend primarily on the memory of its participants and begins to depend on explicit institutional memory.

---

## Context

RESEARCH-001 represents the first checkpoint where a significant portion of the program knowledge transitioned from conversations and personal recollection into version-controlled research artifacts.

---

## Observation

Until this point:

- knowledge lived in conversations;
- implementation details;
- personal memory.

After RESEARCH-001:

- knowledge became versioned;
- reviewable;
- reproducible;
- institutional.

---

## Implication

Institutional memory changes the nature of the program.

The program becomes less dependent on individuals and more dependent on reproducible knowledge.

Checkpoint:

RESEARCH-001
