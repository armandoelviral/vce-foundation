# Summary

The VCE Research Program published its first governed, versioned and reconstructible research methodology.

Methodology became a first-class governed artifact of the Research Program.

---

# Trigger

Successful completion of RC-0002 together with publication of:

- Research Cycle Initiation Framework (RCIF)
- Research Cycle Completion Framework (RCCF)
- Legacy Integration Framework (LIF)
- Methodology Baseline v1.0
- Methodology Versioning Framework

---

# Architectural Significance

Before PM-0001

The Research Program possessed:

- Technology Architecture
- Scientific Objects
- Runtime Governance

After PM-0001

The Research Program additionally possesses:

- Explicit Research Methodology
- Versioned Methodology
- Governed Methodology Evolution
- Research Cycle Lifecycle
- Legacy Governance
- Knowledge Promotion Governance

---

# Program Impact

Methodology is no longer implicit.

Methodology becomes reconstructible.

Methodology becomes versioned.

Methodology becomes governed.

Methodology becomes auditable.

---

# Historical Importance

PM-0001 marks the transition of VCE from a research project into a governed Research Program.
