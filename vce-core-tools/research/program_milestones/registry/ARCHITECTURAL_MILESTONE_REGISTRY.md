# Architectural Milestone Registry (AMR)

## Purpose

Maintain the permanent registry of historical architectural milestones of the VCE Research Program.

Architectural Milestones represent irreversible increases in the architectural capabilities of the Research Program.

They are permanent historical records.

---

# PM-0001

Title

First Methodology Release

Status

Completed

Date

YYYY-MM-DD

Summary

The VCE Research Program published its first governed, versioned and reconstructible research methodology.

Architectural Significance

Methodology became a first-class governed artifact of the Program.

Artifacts

- RCIF
- RCCF
- LIF
- Methodology Baseline v1.0
- Methodology Versioning
- Research Freeze
- Legacy Extraction

Impact

Transition from research project to governed Research Program.

Classification

Historical Architectural Milestone.
