# Legacy Integration Framework (LIF)

## Purpose

Govern the transition from Research Cycle outputs to permanent Research Program knowledge.

The objective is to prevent unvalidated discoveries from becoming permanent architecture.

---

# Sources

A completed Research Cycle may produce:

- Research Freeze
- Final Assessment
- Final Report
- Scientific Contributions
- Legacy Extraction

---

# Integration Process

Research Cycle

↓

Scientific Contributions

↓

Legacy Extraction

↓

Governance Review

↓

Permanent Knowledge Base

---

# Governance Questions

Question 001

Has the contribution survived falsification?

---

Question 002

Does it reduce architectural complexity?

---

Question 003

Does it increase explanatory capability?

---

Question 004

Can future Research Cycles reconstruct it?

---

Question 005

Is it independent from historical context?

---

# Decision

One of:

- Promote to Permanent Knowledge

- Keep as Candidate

- Archive

- Reject

---

# Principle

No contribution becomes permanent knowledge automatically.
