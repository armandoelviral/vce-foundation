# Methodology Versioning Framework

## Purpose

Govern the evolution of the VCE Methodology Baseline.

Methodology shall evolve through explicit versioned baselines rather than undocumented modifications.

---

## Lifecycle

Candidate Methodology

↓

Validation

↓

Governance Review

↓

Baseline Release

↓

Superseded Baseline

---

## Versioning

Major

Breaking methodological changes.

Minor

New validated mechanisms.

Patch

Clarifications.

---

## Rule

Methodology evolves through governed releases.

It never changes implicitly.

---

## Compatibility

Every new baseline shall document:

- preserved mechanisms;
- modified mechanisms;
- deprecated mechanisms;
- migration guidance.

---

## Principle

Methodological history shall remain fully reconstructible.
