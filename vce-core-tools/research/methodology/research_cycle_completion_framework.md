# Research Cycle Completion Framework (RCCF)

## Purpose

Standardize the completion process of every Research Cycle within the VCE Research Program.

Completion is determined by methodological maturity rather than administrative criteria.

---

# Mandatory Completion Artifacts

## Artifact 001

Research Freeze

Purpose

Capture a reconstructible epistemic state.

---

## Artifact 002

Final Assessment

Purpose

Demonstrate methodological sufficiency.

---

## Artifact 003

Final Report

Purpose

Document the complete Research Cycle.

---

## Artifact 004

Scientific Contributions

Purpose

Identify validated contributions produced by the cycle.

---

## Artifact 005

Legacy Extraction

Purpose

Determine which validated knowledge becomes permanent knowledge of the Research Program.

---

# Completion Principle

Research history and permanent knowledge shall remain explicitly separated.

Research history preserves evolution.

Permanent knowledge preserves validated understanding.

---

# Decision Rule

A Research Cycle is considered complete only after all mandatory artifacts have been produced and reviewed.
