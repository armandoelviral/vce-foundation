# Research Cycle Initiation Framework (RCIF)

## Purpose

Standardize how every Research Cycle begins.

The objective is to ensure that every Research Cycle starts from an explicit research need rather than an interesting idea.

---

# Required Inputs

A Research Cycle shall explicitly identify:

- Research Question
- Motivation
- Existing Knowledge
- Expected Contribution
- Success Criteria
- Falsification Strategy
- Exit Criteria

---

# Entry Conditions

A Research Cycle may begin only if:

- the research question is explicitly stated;
- expected contributions are identified;
- a falsification strategy has been defined;
- success criteria are measurable;
- completion criteria are known.

---

# Principle

A Research Cycle begins with a research question—not with a solution.

---

# Output

Approved Research Cycle.
