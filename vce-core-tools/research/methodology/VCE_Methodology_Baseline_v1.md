## Purpose

Define the current official research methodology governing the VCE Research Program.

This document represents the validated methodological baseline after RC-0002 Freeze F1.

# Research Cycle Lifecycle

Every Research Cycle shall follow:

Research Cycle Initiation Framework (RCIF)

↓

Research Execution

↓

Knowledge Capture

↓

Research Freeze

↓

Research Cycle Completion Framework (RCCF)

↓

Legacy Integration Framework (LIF)

↓

Permanent Knowledge Base

---

# Core Methodological Principles

- Counterexample-first research.
- Explicit governance.
- Reconstruction before expansion.
- Architectural reduction before conceptual expansion.
- Separation between Research History and Permanent Knowledge.
- No mechanism is exempt from governance.
- Scientific validity, operational utility and economic sustainability remain explicitly independent.

---

# Mandatory Completion Artifacts

- Research Freeze
- Final Assessment
- Final Report
- Scientific Contributions
- Legacy Extraction

---

# Knowledge Promotion Rule

Knowledge is never promoted automatically.

Promotion requires explicit governance review.

---

# Architectural Objective

Continuously increase explanatory capability while minimizing methodological complexity.
