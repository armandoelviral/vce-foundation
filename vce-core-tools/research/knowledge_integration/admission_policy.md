# Knowledge Admission Policy

## Purpose

The Knowledge Admission Policy defines the conditions under which external scientific knowledge may influence the VCE Research Program.

Admission does not imply acceptance.

Admission authorizes evaluation.

---

# Admission Rules

## Rule KA-001

Every external source shall first become an Evidence Card.

No exceptions.

---

## Rule KA-002

Every Evidence Card shall preserve complete provenance.

Minimum requirements:

- title
- authors
- publication year
- source
- discipline

---

## Rule KA-003

Every Evidence Card shall undergo Comparative Review before influencing internal knowledge.

---

## Rule KA-004

No Design Hypothesis, Scientific Hypothesis, Model or Principle shall be modified directly from a single publication.

Scientific adoption requires Comparative Evidence.

---

## Rule KA-005

Every modification shall preserve:

- rationale
- supporting evidence
- contradictory evidence
- reviewer
- revision date

---

## Rule KA-006

Negative evidence shall be preserved.

Evidence contradicting current hypotheses is considered scientifically valuable.

It shall never be discarded solely because it conflicts with current understanding.

---

## Rule KA-007

Uncertainty shall be explicitly documented.

Absence of evidence is never interpreted as evidence of absence.

---

## Rule KA-008

Every adopted conclusion shall remain reconstructable.

Future researchers shall be able to determine:

- which external sources were considered;
- how they were interpreted;
- why they influenced the research program;
- which competing explanations were rejected.

---

# Admission Outcomes

Every reviewed source shall receive one of the following classifications.

## Accepted for Integration

Suitable for Comparative Evidence.

---

## Accepted with Reservations

Requires additional corroboration.

---

## Deferred

Insufficient evidence.

Requires further review.

---

## Rejected

Methodological or evidentiary deficiencies prevent integration.

Rejection shall preserve provenance.

---

# Guiding Principle

The authority of external knowledge does not originate from publication.

It originates from transparent evaluation, explicit comparison and reconstructable scientific reasoning.

The admission process exists to preserve that distinction.

---

# Long-Term Objective

Knowledge admission shall become a reproducible scientific process rather than an informal research activity.

Every accepted contribution should remain independently auditable by future generations.
