# Knowledge Integration Pipeline

## Purpose

The Knowledge Integration Pipeline defines the controlled process through which external scientific knowledge becomes part of the VCE Research Program.

The pipeline intentionally separates:

- observation
- comparison
- interpretation
- adoption

This prevents direct incorporation of external claims into the internal scientific corpus.

# Stage Descriptions

## Stage 1 — External Source

Scientific information originates outside the VCE Research Program.

Examples include:

- journal articles
- books
- conference proceedings
- standards
- RFCs
- technical reports
- dissertations
- reference implementations

At this stage the source has no direct authority inside the program.

---

## Stage 2 — Evidence Card

The source is normalized into a common structure.

The objective is to preserve:

- provenance
- research problem
- methodology
- evidence
- conclusions

without introducing interpretation.

---

## Stage 3 — Comparative Literature Review

Multiple Evidence Cards are compared.

The objective is to determine:

- conceptual overlap
- methodological similarity
- contradictions
- gaps
- possible novelty

No hypothesis is modified during this stage.

---

## Stage 4 — Comparative Evidence

The review produces Comparative Evidence.

Comparative Evidence summarizes what the literature collectively supports or contradicts.

This becomes the first input capable of influencing internal research.

---

## Stage 5 — Research Review

Researchers evaluate whether Comparative Evidence affects:

- Design Hypotheses
- Scientific Hypotheses
- Models
- Formalizations

Every decision shall be explicitly documented.

---

## Stage 6 — Knowledge Integration

Only after review may internal artifacts be updated.

Every modification shall preserve:

- provenance
- rationale
- evidence
- reviewer
- date

---

# Guiding Principle

The research program never adopts literature.

The research program adopts conclusions justified through explicit comparative evaluation.

This distinction preserves scientific transparency.

---

# Long-Term Objective

Every accepted principle shall remain reconstructable back to the complete chain of external scientific evidence that justified its adoption.
