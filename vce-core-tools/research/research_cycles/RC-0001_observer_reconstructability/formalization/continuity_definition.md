# Purpose

Provide an operational definition of continuity suitable for logical experimentation.

This definition is provisional.

Its purpose is to enable systematic evaluation rather than establish constitutional knowledge.

---

# Initial Observation

The execution of CE-001 demonstrated that the term "continuity" is insufficiently specified.

A more precise operational definition is therefore required.

---

# Candidate Definitions

## Definition A

Continuity as Historical Preservation

Definition

The complete history of a system remains reconstructable.

Evaluation Focus

History.

---

## Definition B

Continuity as Semantic Preservation

Definition

Successive observers can reconstruct the intended meaning of preserved history.

Evaluation Focus

Meaning.

---

## Definition C

Continuity as Functional Preservation

Definition

Future generations can continue operating the system while preserving its governing principles.

Evaluation Focus

Operational capability.

---

## Definition D

Continuity as Identity Preservation

Definition

The system remains recognizably the same system despite internal evolution.

Evaluation Focus

Identity.

---

# Current Status

No definition has been adopted.

EXP-0002 shall evaluate each definition independently.

The experiment shall determine whether reconstructability is sufficient under any of these definitions.
