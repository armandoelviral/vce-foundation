# Emerging Pattern

Current evidence suggests a recurring pattern.

Engineering organizations frequently define:

- explicit maturity levels;
- promotion pathways;
- review processes;
- revision mechanisms.

However, these mechanisms appear to remain domain-specific.

At the current stage of the experiment, no reviewed framework has been observed to define a generalized epistemic contract applicable across heterogeneous scientific knowledge artifacts.

This observation remains provisional.
