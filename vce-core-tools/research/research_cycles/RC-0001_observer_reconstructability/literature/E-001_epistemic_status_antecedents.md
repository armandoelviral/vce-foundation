# E-001 Literature Review — Epistemic Status Contract Antecedents

## Purpose

This review searches for existing frameworks that may already implement the same function as the proposed VCE Epistemic Status Contract.

The objective is to challenge novelty, not defend it.

## Preliminary Interpretation

Existing frameworks cover parts of the proposed mechanism.

However, no equivalence is established yet.

The key question is whether any framework requires every knowledge artifact to declare both:

1. its epistemic status, and
2. the validation conditions required for status transition.

## Current Finding

Preliminary status:

Partial antecedents likely exist.

Full equivalence not yet established.

No novelty claim is allowed at this stage.
