# Dependency Analysis

## Purpose

The purpose of this phase is to determine whether the candidate conditions identified during EXP-0002 constitute an irreducible explanatory basis for long-term knowledge continuity.

Rather than searching for additional conditions, this phase attempts to eliminate unnecessary ones.

Scientific progress is therefore measured by explanatory simplification rather than conceptual expansion.

---

# Scientific Question

Can any candidate condition be logically derived from another?

If a condition can always be derived from another, it shall not remain part of the minimal explanatory basis.

---

# Working Principle

Every candidate condition is considered provisional.

No condition possesses permanent status.

Each remains subject to elimination through logical analysis or counterexample.

---

# Scientific Strategy

The analysis follows the sequence:

Candidate Conditions

↓

Logical Dependency Analysis

↓

Counterexample Search

↓

Minimal Independent Basis

↓

Architectural Theory

---

# Expected Outputs

This phase shall produce:

- dependency matrices;
- independence analyses;
- logical reduction reports;
- architectural refinement;
- evidence supporting or rejecting candidate conditions.

---

# Success Criterion

The phase is considered successful when no remaining candidate condition can be eliminated without reducing the explanatory power of the resulting theory.

---

# Relationship to EXP-0002

EXP-0002 identifies candidate conditions.

Dependency Analysis evaluates whether those conditions are genuinely independent.

Together they progressively construct the minimal architecture explaining knowledge continuity.
