# Purpose

The objective of this document is to define, before reviewing the literature, what evidence would be considered sufficient to falsify DH-002.

This prevents retrospective reinterpretation of evidence.

The falsification criteria themselves become part of the permanent research record.

---

# Hypothesis Under Evaluation

DH-002

Epistemic Status Contract

Research Question:

Can explicit epistemic status declarations improve the reconstructability and auditability of scientific knowledge?

---

# Falsification Criteria

DH-002 shall be considered fully falsified if an existing framework already requires every scientific knowledge artifact to explicitly declare:

- epistemic category;
- maturity level;
- validation status;
- evidence status;
- review status;
- revision history;
- promotion conditions.

The framework must also define explicit transition rules between epistemic states.

---

# Partial Falsification

The hypothesis shall be partially falsified if multiple existing frameworks collectively provide all of the above capabilities.

In that case the contribution of VCE would shift from invention to integration.

---

# Non-Falsifying Evidence

The following shall NOT constitute falsification:

- similar terminology;
- isolated maturity models;
- provenance models;
- workflow systems;
- documentation standards.

Similarity alone is insufficient.

Functional equivalence must be demonstrated.
