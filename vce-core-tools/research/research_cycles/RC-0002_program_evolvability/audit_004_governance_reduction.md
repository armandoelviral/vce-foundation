# RC2-AUDIT-004

# Governance Reduction

## Purpose

Determine whether Governance is an irreducible architectural primitive or merely a composition of lower-level decision mechanisms.

---

## Candidate Reduction

Policies

+

Decision Rules

+

Evaluation

↓

Governance

---

## Question 001

Can Governance be completely represented as policy evaluation?

Status

Open.

---

## Question 002

Does Governance perform behavior beyond evaluating explicit rules?

Status

Open.

---

## Question 003

Can every Governance decision be represented as deterministic policy execution?

Status

Open.

---

## Possible Outcomes

Outcome A

Governance disappears.

Outcome B

Governance becomes a specialization.

Outcome C

Governance remains irreducible.

---

## Decision Rule

Governance survives only if it explains behavior that cannot be represented by policy evaluation alone.	

---

# Reduction Test GOV-RT-001

## Target

Governance

---

## Candidate Reduction

Governance = Policy Evaluation

---

## Test Case

A policy states:

Every Candidate Theory must pass Derivation Protocol before admission.

---

## Policy Evaluation Result

The policy can determine whether the condition is satisfied.

---

## Failure Case

Policy Evaluation alone does not explain:

- who defines policies;
- how policy conflicts are resolved;
- how exceptions are handled;
- how policies are revised;
- how authority is assigned;
- how competing admissible actions are prioritized.

---

## Result

Complete reduction fails.

Governance cannot currently be reduced to Policy Evaluation alone.

---

## Revised Candidate Reduction

Governance may be reducible to:

Policy Evaluation

+

Conflict Resolution

+

Authority Model

+

Policy Revision Mechanism

---

## Status

Governance remains provisionally irreducible until the revised reduction is tested.
