# Method Boundary Audit

## Purpose

Determine the conditions under which the methodological architecture should stop increasing in complexity.

---

## Research Question

Can the methodological architecture reach a stable state beyond which additional mechanisms are no longer justified?

---

## Evaluation Questions

Question 001

Does a proposed mechanism eliminate an existing one?

---

Question 002

Does it increase explanatory capability?

---

Question 003

Does it reduce architectural complexity?

---

Question 004

Can its function be parameterized into an existing mechanism?

---

## Candidate Boundary

The methodological architecture shall cease expanding when every newly proposed mechanism fails to produce net architectural simplification.

---

## Decision Rule

Method evolution is justified only by measurable improvement in explanatory efficiency or architectural economy.
