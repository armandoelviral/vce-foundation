# Hostile Domain Campaign

## Purpose

Identify research domains most likely to invalidate the generalized Reduction methodology.

The objective is not successful application.

The objective is maximal methodological stress.

---

# Selection Criteria

Domains shall maximize one or more of:

- ambiguity;
- distributed authority;
- conflicting evidence;
- evolutionary instability;
- semantic drift;
- absence of formal structure.

---

# Candidate Domains

## Mathematics

Threat

Reduction may be unnecessary because formal proof already governs acceptance.

---

## Constitutional Law

Threat

Competing interpretations may prevent convergence.

---

## Medicine

Threat

Evidence is continuously revised and often contradictory.

---

## Linux Kernel

Threat

Highly distributed governance with historical path dependence.

---

## Kubernetes

Threat

Architecture evolves through community consensus.

---

## Evolutionary Biology

Threat

Historical contingency may prevent unique reduction.

---

## Economics

Threat

Competing schools of thought coexist indefinitely.

---

## Decision Rule

Domains shall be prioritized by expected probability of methodological failure.
