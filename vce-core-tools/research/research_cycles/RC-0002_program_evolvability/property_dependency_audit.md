# Property Dependency Audit

## Purpose

Determine whether the candidate architectural properties are independent or derivable.

---

## Candidate Properties

- Reconstruction
- Identity Preservation
- Dependency Closure
- Convergence
- Foundation Uniqueness

---

## Evaluation

For every pair of properties determine:

- Independence
- Mutual implication
- Partial implication
- Derivability
- Circular dependency

---

## Research Questions

Question 001

Does Reconstruction require Identity Preservation?

---

Question 002

Does Convergence imply Foundation Uniqueness?

---

Question 003

Can Dependency Closure be derived from Reconstruction?

---

Question 004

Which properties are fundamental?

---

## Decision Rule

Architectural properties shall be minimized using the same reduction principles applied to concepts.
