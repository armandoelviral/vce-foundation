# Architecture Layers Audit

## Purpose

Determine whether the complete VCE Research Program can be reconstructed from a minimal set of architectural layers.

---

## Candidate Layers

- Technology
- Knowledge
- Methodology
- Governance
- Evidence

---

## Evaluation Questions

Question 001

Can every major VCE component be assigned to one primary architectural layer?

---

Question 002

Do the layers minimize cross-layer dependencies?

---

Question 003

Can the complete Research Program be reconstructed using only these layers and their governed interactions?

---

Question 004

Does introducing additional layers increase explanatory capability?

---

## Success Criterion

The candidate layer architecture reconstructs the complete VCE Research Program while minimizing architectural complexity.

---

## Decision Rule

Architectural layers shall be accepted only if they increase explanatory clarity without introducing unnecessary organizational complexity.
