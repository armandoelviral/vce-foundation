# Mission Falsification Campaign

## Purpose

Attempt to demonstrate that the central mission of the VCE Research Program is internally inconsistent.

---

# Mission

Scientific Objects shall generate knowledge, products, services, validation mechanisms and resources sufficient to sustain the continued evolution of the Research Program.

---

## Attack MF-001

Economic Circularity

Question

Can the Research Program produce enough value to finance itself?

Failure

Permanent dependence upon external funding.

---

## Attack MF-002

Scientific Circularity

Question

Can Scientific Objects generate genuinely new Scientific Objects?

Failure

Knowledge production eventually stagnates.

---

## Attack MF-003

Methodological Circularity

Question

Can the mechanisms governing Scientific Objects themselves evolve without producing infinite regress?

Failure

Methodological explosion.

---

## Attack MF-004

Validation Circularity

Question

Can the program objectively validate itself without external validation?

Failure

Self-confirming methodology.

---

## Attack MF-005

Commercial Circularity

Question

Can Scientific Products generate revenue without gradually replacing scientific objectives with commercial optimization?

Failure

Mission drift.

---

## Attack MF-006

Knowledge Saturation

Question

Does knowledge growth eventually stop producing meaningful explanatory improvement?

Failure

Scientific Objects become archival rather than generative.

---

## Success Criterion

The mission survives all attacks without internal contradiction.
