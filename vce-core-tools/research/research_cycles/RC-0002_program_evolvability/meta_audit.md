# Meta Audit

## Purpose

Evaluate whether methodological mechanisms remain valid when applied to themselves.

The objective is to identify recursive inconsistencies.

---

## Candidate Mechanisms

- Governance
- Derivation Protocol
- Theory Admission Protocol
- Architectural Reduction Protocol

---

## Meta Question 001

Can the mechanism evaluate itself?

---

## Meta Question 002

Does self-application introduce logical contradictions?

---

## Meta Question 003

Can self-application improve the mechanism?

---

## Decision Rule

A methodological mechanism should not be exempt from the same evaluation process that it imposes on the Research Program.
