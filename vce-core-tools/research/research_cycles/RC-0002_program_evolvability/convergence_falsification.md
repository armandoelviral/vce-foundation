# Convergence Falsification

## Purpose

Attempt to falsify the claim that repeated methodological reduction converges toward a stable Minimal Architectural Foundation.

---

## Target Claim

Repeated application of Reduction over the same validated evidence eventually stabilizes.

---

## Falsification Scenario 001

### Oscillation

Reduction alternates between two or more architectural foundations without stabilizing.

Example:

Foundation A

↓

Reduction

↓

Foundation B

↓

Reduction

↓

Foundation A

---

## Falsification Scenario 002

### Infinite Descent

Every reduction cycle produces another reducible foundation.

No stable stopping point emerges.

---

## Falsification Scenario 003

### Evidence Reclassification

The same evidence is repeatedly reclassified, producing different foundations.

---

## Falsification Scenario 004

### Rule Sensitivity

Small changes in reduction rules produce incompatible foundations.

---

## Falsification Scenario 005

### Observer Sensitivity

Different researchers applying the same rules produce incompatible foundations.

---

## Failure Condition for Convergence

Convergence fails if any scenario above is demonstrated with validated evidence.
