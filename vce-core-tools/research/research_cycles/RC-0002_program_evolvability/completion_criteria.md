# RC-0002 Completion Criteria

## Purpose

Define objective conditions under which RC-0002 may be considered complete.

Completion shall never depend upon subjective judgment.

---

## Criterion C-001

All candidate concepts have undergone Derivation Protocol.

---

## Criterion C-002

All surviving concepts have undergone Theory Admission.

---

## Criterion C-003

All surviving concepts have undergone Architectural Reduction.

---

## Criterion C-004

All remaining primitives have explicit dependency graphs.

---

## Criterion C-005

All composite structures possess explicit composition rules.

---

## Criterion C-006

The proposed foundation successfully reconstructs the complete Research Program.

---

## Criterion C-007

No additional complete reduction cycle produces further architectural simplification.

---

## Criterion C-008

Counterexample Campaigns fail to invalidate the proposed foundation.

---

## Completion Rule

RC-0002 is complete only when every criterion above has been satisfied.
