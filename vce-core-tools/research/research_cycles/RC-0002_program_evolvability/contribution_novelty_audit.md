# Contribution Novelty Audit

## Purpose

Evaluate whether each candidate contribution of RC-0002 represents:

- a new contribution;
- a synthesis of existing knowledge;
- a specialization of existing ideas;
- or an unnecessary reformulation.

Novelty shall never be assumed.

---

## Classification

### Type A

Novel architectural contribution.

---

### Type B

Novel integration of existing concepts.

---

### Type C

Specialization.

---

### Type D

Reformulation.
