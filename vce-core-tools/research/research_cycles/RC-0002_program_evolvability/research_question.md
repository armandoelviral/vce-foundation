# Research Question

## ID

RQ-RC0002-001

---

## Question

Can Research Program Evolvability be fully derived from existing Scientific Object Theory, Corpus Theory and Governance without introducing additional theoretical constructs?

---

## Motivation

The VCE Research Program has produced candidate mechanisms such as TAP, ARP, Scientific Objects and Corpus governance.

RC-0002 investigates whether these mechanisms require a distinct theory of Research Program Evolvability or whether they can be explained by existing components.

---

## Falsification Orientation

The cycle assumes, as a starting discipline, that a new theory is unnecessary.

The burden of proof lies on demonstrating irreducibility.

---

## Expected Outcomes

One of three outcomes is expected:

1. Research Program Evolvability is fully derivable from existing theories.

2. Research Program Evolvability is partially derivable but requires additional constructs.

3. Research Program Evolvability is irreducible and deserves candidate theory status.
