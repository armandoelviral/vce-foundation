# RC-0002 — Research Program Evolvability

## Purpose

RC-0002 investigates whether Research Program Evolvability requires an independent theory or can be derived from existing VCE components.

The cycle begins by attempting to prove that no new theory is needed.

---

## Central Question

Can a research program systematically improve the mechanisms through which it produces, evaluates, selects and preserves knowledge while maintaining conceptual continuity?

---

## Initial Strategy

RC-0002 shall not begin by proposing a new theory.

It shall begin by attempting to eliminate the need for one.

---

## Core Challenge

Research Program Evolvability must demonstrate that it cannot be fully reduced to:

- Scientific Object Theory
- Corpus Theory
- Governance
- Existing Research Operations

---

## Method

The cycle will proceed through:

1. Reasons we might be wrong
2. Derivation attempts
3. Architectural reduction
4. Comparative program analysis
5. Counterexample search
6. Decision

---

## Success Criterion

RC-0002 survives only if Research Program Evolvability cannot be fully derived from existing theories without loss of explanatory power.

---

## Status

Active.

---

# Secondary Objective

Beyond evaluating Research Program Evolvability, RC-0002 shall attempt to identify the smallest irreducible architectural foundation capable of explaining all validated phenomena produced by the VCE Research Program.

The objective is not conceptual reduction alone.

The objective is architectural minimization while preserving explanatory completeness.
