# RC2-AUDIT-003

## Purpose

Determine whether all methodological mechanisms belong to a single architectural category ("Protocol") or whether multiple irreducible mechanism classes exist.

---

## Candidate Mechanisms

- Governance
- DP
- TAP
- ARP
- KCP

---

## Question 001

Are all of these mechanisms actually protocols?

Status

Open.

---

## Question 002

Can they instead be classified into smaller architectural categories?

Status

Open.

---

## Candidate Categories

- Decision Mechanism
- Analytical Method
- Audit Procedure
- Lifecycle Transition
- Governance Rule

These categories are only hypotheses.

---

## Decision Rule

The smallest classification capable of explaining every mechanism shall be preferred.

No new category shall survive unless it proves irreducible.
