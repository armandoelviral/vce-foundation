# Foundation Uniqueness Audit

## Purpose

Determine whether the Minimal Architectural Foundation is unique.

---

## Research Question

Can two or more irreducible architectural foundations explain the same validated phenomena with equivalent explanatory power?

---

## Candidate Outcomes

Outcome A

A unique foundation exists.

---

Outcome B

Multiple equivalent minimal foundations exist.

---

Outcome C

Foundation uniqueness cannot be determined from available evidence.

---

## Evaluation

For each candidate foundation evaluate:

- explanatory coverage;
- dependency structure;
- reconstruction capability;
- architectural complexity.

---

## Decision Rule

Uniqueness shall never be assumed.

It must emerge from comparative evidence.
