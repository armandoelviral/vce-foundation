# Reduction Test RT-001

## Target

Theory Admission Protocol

---

## Reduction Attempt

Attempt to reduce TAP to Governance.

---

## Governance Explanation

Governance can evaluate whether a proposed operation is admissible under existing policies.

---

## TAP Explanation

TAP evaluates whether a proposed theoretical abstraction deserves independent status within the Research Program.

---

## Comparison

Governance decides whether an operation is allowed.

TAP decides whether a new theoretical construct is necessary.

These are related but not identical.

---

## Result

Partial Reduction.

TAP uses governance mechanisms, but its object of evaluation is different.

Governance evaluates operations.

TAP evaluates candidate theories.

---

## Unexplained Residue

Governance alone does not currently explain:

- theory necessity;
- derivability testing;
- conceptual redundancy;
- theoretical admission criteria.

---

## Decision

TAP cannot yet be fully reduced to Governance.

Status:

Provisionally Irreducible.
