# Foundation Candidates

## Purpose

Maintain the current candidate architectural foundation of the VCE Research Program.

No candidate shall be considered primitive until it survives all reduction, derivation and reconstruction audits.

---

## Candidate Foundation Set

- Reality
- Identity
- Representation
- State
- Event
- Operation
- Transition

---

## Under Audit

- Governance
- Scientific Object
- Corpus
- Research Cycle

---

## Excluded

None.

---

## Decision Rule

Candidates shall move between sections as evidence accumulates.

The objective is not to maximize the number of primitives.

The objective is to minimize the primitive foundation while preserving complete architectural reconstructability.
