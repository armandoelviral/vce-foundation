# Meta Reduction Self Audit

## Purpose

Determine whether the generalized Reduction operator is itself irreducible.

---

## Target

Reduction(Entity, Rules, Evidence, DecisionVocabulary)

---

## Research Questions

Question 001

Can Reduction itself be derived from more primitive operations?

---

Question 002

Is Reduction actually composed of multiple independent operators?

---

Question 003

Can different generalized Reduction operators explain the same observations?

---

Question 004

Can Reduction be eliminated without reducing explanatory capability?

---

## Success Criterion

Reduction survives systematic derivation, decomposition and architectural reduction.

---

## Failure Criterion

Reduction is shown to be decomposable or derivable from a smaller mechanism.
