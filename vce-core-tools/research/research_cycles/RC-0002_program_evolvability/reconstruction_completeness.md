# Reconstruction Completeness Audit

## Purpose

Determine whether the Minimal Architectural Foundation is sufficient to reconstruct the original Research Program without loss of architectural meaning.

---

## Reconstruction Pipeline

Original Architecture

↓

Reduction

↓

Primitive Set

↓

Dependency Graph

↓

Transformation Rules

↓

Reconstructed Architecture

---

## Evaluation Questions

Question 001

Is every architectural component reconstructable?

---

Question 002

Does reconstruction preserve conceptual identity?

---

Question 003

Does reconstruction introduce additional concepts?

---

Question 004

Does reconstruction lose explanatory capability?

---

## Success Criterion

The reconstructed architecture shall be architecturally equivalent to the original architecture.

---

## Failure Criterion

Any loss of identity, explanatory capability or dependency completeness invalidates the proposed primitive foundation.
