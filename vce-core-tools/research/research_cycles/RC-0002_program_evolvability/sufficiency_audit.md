# RC-0002 Sufficiency Audit

## Purpose

Determine whether RC-0002 has produced sufficient validated knowledge to justify closure.

Closure shall be based on methodological sufficiency rather than exhaustion of possible research questions.

---

## Evaluation Criteria

### Criterion 001

Has the Research Question been answered?

---

### Criterion 002

Have the principal hypotheses survived systematic falsification?

---

### Criterion 003

Have the surviving discoveries been reduced to an irreducible explanatory core?

---

### Criterion 004

Does the resulting methodology reconstruct the complete Research Cycle?

---

### Criterion 005

Are remaining open questions extensions rather than blockers?

---

## Closure Decision

One of:

- Ready for Closure
- Additional Validation Required
- Insufficient Evidence

---

## Decision Rule

A Research Cycle is complete when additional investigation is expected to produce diminishing architectural returns relative to its original research objective.
