# Architecture Layer Audit

## Purpose

Determine whether the architectural elements of the VCE Research Program belong to distinct abstraction layers or form a single undifferentiated model.

---

## Candidate Layers

Layer A

Architectural Primitives

---

Layer B

Operations and Composition Mechanisms

---

Layer C

Composite Structures

---

Layer D

Governed Systems

---

## Research Questions

Question 001

Can every architectural element be assigned to exactly one abstraction layer?

---

Question 002

Can abstraction layers be reduced?

---

Question 003

Are composition rules valid across multiple layers?

---

Question 004

Does layer separation reduce conceptual ambiguity?

---

## Decision Rule

Architectural layers shall be introduced only if they reduce ambiguity and increase explanatory power.

Otherwise they shall be rejected.
