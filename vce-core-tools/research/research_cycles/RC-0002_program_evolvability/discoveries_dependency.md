# Discovery Dependency Audit

## Purpose

Determine whether the candidate discoveries of RC-0002 are independent or derivable.

Scientific discoveries shall be minimized using the same methodological discipline applied to concepts, theories and architectural mechanisms.

---

## Candidate Discoveries

- Governed Architectural Transformation
- Mission Decoupling
- Generalized Reduction
- Governed Decoupling
- Methodological Governance
- Research Freeze

---

## Evaluation

For every pair of discoveries determine:

- Independent
- Derivable
- Partially Derivable
- Mutually Reinforcing

---

## Research Questions

Question 001

Does Governed Decoupling derive from Generalized Reduction?

---

Question 002

Does Research Freeze derive from Methodological Governance?

---

Question 003

Is Mission Decoupling a consequence of Governed Decoupling?

---

Question 004

Which discoveries remain fundamental after dependency reduction?

---

## Success Criterion

The final contribution set contains only irreducible discoveries.

---

## Decision Rule

A discovery shall remain fundamental only if it cannot be reconstructed from the remaining discoveries.
