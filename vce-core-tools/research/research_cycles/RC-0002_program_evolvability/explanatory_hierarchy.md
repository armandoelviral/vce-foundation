# Explanatory Hierarchy Audit

## Purpose

Determine the explanatory hierarchy among the surviving discoveries of RC-0002.

The objective is to identify which discoveries are fundamental and which emerge as consequences.

---

## Candidate Fundamental Discoveries

- Generalized Reduction
- Methodological Governance

---

## Candidate Emergent Discoveries

- Governed Decoupling
- Mission Decoupling
- Research Freeze
- Architectural Transformation

---

## Evaluation

For each discovery determine:

- explains another discovery;
- depends upon another discovery;
- mutually reinforces another discovery;
- remains independent.

---

## Success Criterion

Every surviving discovery occupies a justified position within the explanatory hierarchy.

---

## Decision Rule

The hierarchy shall minimize the number of fundamental discoveries while preserving explanatory completeness.ç
