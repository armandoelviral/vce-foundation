# Convergence Hypothesis

## ID

H-RC0002-001

---

## Hypothesis

Repeated application of the VCE methodological mechanisms
(DP, TAP, ARP and Governance)

over the same validated evidence

will converge toward the same Minimal Architectural Foundation.

---

## Required Conditions

- Same evidence
- Same reduction rules
- Same governance rules
- Same derivation rules

---

## Prediction

Independent researchers applying the methodology should obtain architecturally equivalent foundations.

---

## Falsification

The hypothesis is falsified if independent research groups,
using equivalent evidence and methodology,
converge toward incompatible architectural foundations.

---

## Status

Candidate.
