# Foundation Non-Uniqueness Campaign

## Purpose

Attempt to demonstrate that multiple distinct Minimal Architectural Foundations can explain the same validated Research Program.

---

## Target Claim

The Minimal Architectural Foundation is unique.

---

## Counterexample Strategy 001

Construct two distinct primitive sets that:

- explain the same validated phenomena;
- reconstruct the same Research Program;
- exhibit equivalent explanatory capability.

---

## Counterexample Strategy 002

Construct two foundations with different dependency graphs but equivalent reconstruction.

---

## Counterexample Strategy 003

Construct two foundations differing only in primitive decomposition.

---

## Counterexample Strategy 004

Demonstrate that multiple parameterizations of Reduction converge to different minimal foundations.

---

## Evaluation

For every candidate foundation compare:

- explanatory coverage;
- reconstruction capability;
- dependency complexity;
- semantic consistency;
- methodological economy.

---

## Failure Condition

If two architecturally distinct foundations satisfy every evaluation criterion, uniqueness is falsified.
