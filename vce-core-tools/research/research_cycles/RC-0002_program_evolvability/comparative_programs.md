# Comparative Reduction Analysis

## Purpose

Attempt to reduce TAP and ARP to existing Governance.

If successful, no independent protocols are required.

---

# Comparative Analysis

## Candidate Protocol

Theory Admission Protocol (TAP)

---

### Reduction Attempt

Candidate Mapping

Governance

↓

Admission Decision

↓

Theory Accepted

Question

Does Governance already perform every function required by TAP?

---

## Candidate Protocol

Architectural Reduction Protocol (ARP)

---

### Reduction Attempt

Candidate Mapping

Governance

↓

Periodic Review

↓

Architecture Updated

Question

Can Governance periodically remove unnecessary concepts without introducing ARP?

---

## Evaluation Criteria

For each protocol determine:

1. Complete Reduction
2. Partial Reduction
3. No Reduction

---

## Decision Rule

If TAP and ARP can both be completely represented by Governance, they shall be removed as independent protocols.

Otherwise, remaining unexplained behavior shall be documented.
