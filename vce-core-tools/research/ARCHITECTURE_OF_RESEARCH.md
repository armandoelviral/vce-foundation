# Purpose

This document describes the architectural
organization of the VCE Research Institute.

It defines the major layers through which
research questions evolve into institutional
knowledge.

The document does not prescribe architecture.

It documents the architecture that has
emerged from the Scientific Product Program.

---

# Architectural Principle

Every architectural layer exists to
institutionalize the production of the
layer immediately below it.

# Layer 1

Research Institute

Object of Study

The organization of scientific research.

Produces

Research disciplines.

Institutionalizes

Long-term scientific direction.

Evolution

Through governance,
institutional memory
and strategic research.

---

# Layer 2

Discipline

Object of Study

A coherent scientific domain.

Produces

Research Programs.

Institutionalizes

Theory and scientific vocabulary.

Evolution

Through recurring investigations,
evidence accumulation and refinement.

---

# Layer 3

Research Program

Object of Study

A specific scientific problem.

Produces

Scientific Products.

Institutionalizes

Research questions,
observations,
hypotheses
and principles.

Evolution

Through implementation,
Scientific Destruction
and evidence.

---

# Layer 4

Scientific Product

Object of Study

Institutional capabilities.

Produces

Operational capabilities.

Institutionalizes

Organizational learning.

Evolution

Through Runtime,
Evidence
and Validation.

---

# Layer 5

Capability

Object of Study

Repeatable organizational behavior.

Produces

Reliable organizational outcomes.

Institutionalizes

Knowledge in action.

Evolution

Through repeated execution,
measurement
and refinement.

---

# Layer 6

Runtime

Object of Study

Deterministic execution.

Produces

Artifacts.

Institutionalizes

Correct execution.

Evolution

Through implementation,
verification
and replay.

---

# Layer 7

Artifact

Object of Study

Observable evidence.

Produces

Institutional memory.

Institutionalizes

Traceability.

Evolution

Through version control.

# Cross-Cutting Systems

The following systems span every layer.

• Human-AI Standards

• Knowledge Governance

• Institutional Memory

• Scientific Destruction

• Evidence

• Version Control

These systems ensure coherence across
the complete research architecture.

how reproducible systems generate:

- reproducible software;

- reproducible capabilities;

- reproducible knowledge;

- reproducible research institutes.
