# Daily Research Session Protocol

Every research session follows the same execution sequence.

---

## Step 1

Observe Program State

Read:

- Observatory
- Registry
- Current Research Cycle

---

## Step 2

Select Current Research Question

Exactly one primary objective.

---

## Step 3

Review Existing Evidence

No theory expansion before evidence review.

---

## Step 4

Perform Research

Possible activities:

- literature review
- formalization
- experiment
- implementation
- comparison
- falsification

---

## Step 5

Update Research Artifacts

Possible updates:

- Registry
- Knowledge Graph
- Roadmap
- Observatory
- Metrics

---

## Step 6

Generate Session Summary

Document:

- observations
- evidence
- revisions
- remaining uncertainty

---

## Step 7

Define Next Session

Every session must leave a well-defined next objective.
