# Research Hypotheses

## H0 — Intent-to-Understanding Continuity

The continuity between original intent and future understanding can be preserved with higher fidelity through verifiable evidence than through artifacts alone.

## H1 — Answerability

Answerability is an emergent property of high-fidelity decision reconstruction.

## H2 — Context Loss

As information becomes abundant, knowledge degradation is increasingly caused by context loss rather than information loss.

## H3 — Evidence Bundles

Evidence bundles outperform document-only archives when reconstructing historical decisions.

## H4 — Replayable Provenance

Replayable provenance improves historical reconstruction fidelity.
