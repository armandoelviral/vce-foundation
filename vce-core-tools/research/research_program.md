# VCE Research Program

## Central Research Question

How can the continuity between original intent and future understanding be preserved through verifiable evidence?

## Central Hypothesis

The continuity between original intent and future understanding can be preserved with higher fidelity by verifiably preserving the relationships among intent, context, evidence, provenance, policy, and execution than by preserving artifacts alone.

## Null Hypothesis

Preserving additional contextual and provenance information does not significantly improve future reconstruction fidelity compared with preserving artifacts alone.

## Observable Variables

- Reconstruction fidelity
- Reconstruction time
- Context completeness
- Policy recoverability
- Decision explainability
- Interpretation consistency
- Answerability rate
