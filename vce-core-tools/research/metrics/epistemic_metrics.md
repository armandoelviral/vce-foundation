# Epistemic Metrics

The purpose of these metrics is to evaluate how scientific knowledge evolves.

---

## EM-001

Hypotheses Created

Definition:

Number of new hypotheses introduced.

---

## EM-002

Hypotheses Refuted

Definition:

Number of hypotheses rejected after evidence.

Objective:

Measure scientific self-correction.

---

## EM-003

Hypotheses Revised

Definition:

Hypotheses modified after criticism or evidence.

---

## EM-004

Research Questions Closed

Definition:

Research questions satisfactorily answered.

---

## EM-005

Research Questions Reopened

Definition:

Questions reopened after new evidence.

---

## EM-006

Counterexamples Preserved

Definition:

Counterexamples formally documented.

---

## EM-007

Literature Reviews Completed

Definition:

Completed comparative literature reviews.

---

## EM-008

Formalizations Produced

Definition:

Mathematical or logical formalizations.

---

## EM-009

Independent Replications

Definition:

Independent validations.

---

## EM-010

Constitutional Revisions

Definition:

Constitutional knowledge updated through explicit governance.

---

## Objective

The objective is not maximizing numbers.

The objective is maximizing scientific legitimacy.
