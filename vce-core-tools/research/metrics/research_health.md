# Research Health Indicators

The purpose of these indicators is to evaluate whether the research program remains scientifically healthy.

---

## RH-001

Refuted Hypothesis Ratio

Purpose:

Measure willingness to reject incorrect ideas.

---

## RH-002

Average Time Until First Counterexample

Purpose:

Evaluate critical responsiveness.

---

## RH-003

Literature Review Coverage

Definition:

Percentage of hypotheses with completed literature review.

---

## RH-004

Formalization Coverage

Definition:

Percentage of hypotheses formally modeled.

---

## RH-005

Evidence Coverage

Definition:

Percentage of hypotheses supported by explicit evidence.

---

## RH-006

Average Review Depth

Definition:

Average number of review iterations.

---

## RH-007

Research Cycle Completion

Definition:

Completed Research Cycles over total initiated.

---

## RH-008

Evidence Diversity Index

Evidence sources include:

- Experimental
- Comparative
- Operational
- Formal

The objective is to avoid dependence on a single evidence source.
