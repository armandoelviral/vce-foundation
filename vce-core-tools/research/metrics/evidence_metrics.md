# Evidence Metrics

Evidence shall be classified according to its origin.

---

## Experimental Evidence (EE)

Generated through experiments.

---

## Comparative Evidence (CE)

Generated through literature comparison.

---

## Operational Evidence (OE)

Generated through operational deployments.

---

## Formal Evidence (FE)

Generated through mathematical proofs, model checking or formal verification.

---

## Objective

Evidence diversity strengthens scientific confidence.

No single evidence type should dominate the research program.
