# Research Cycle Operating Protocol

Every research session shall begin by declaring:

---

Research Cycle:

Current Milestone:

Current Research Question:

Current Design Hypothesis:

Current Scientific Hypothesis:

Current Evidence:

Current Literature Review:

Current Formalization:

Current Objective:

Expected Deliverable:

---

During the session every change shall be classified as:

- Observation (OBS)
- Research Question (RQ)
- Design Hypothesis (DH)
- Scientific Hypothesis (H)
- Model (M)
- Formalization (F)
- Evidence (E)
- Counterexample (CE)
- Accepted Principle (P)
- Constitutional Decision (CD)

No new category may be introduced without explicit justification.

---

Every session shall finish by recording:

Artifacts Updated

Evidence Generated

Hypotheses Modified

Questions Opened

Questions Closed

Knowledge Graph Updated

Registry Updated

Roadmap Updated

Next Session Objective
