# Scientific Corpus

## Purpose

The Scientific Corpus is the governed knowledge layer of the VCE Research Program.

Its responsibility is not to store documents.

Its responsibility is to govern the lifecycle of Scientific Objects.

Scientific Objects preserve:

- identity;
- provenance;
- epistemic state;
- lifecycle;
- relationships;
- evidence;
- historical evolution.

Documents are considered representations of Scientific Objects.

---

## Responsibilities

The Corpus governs:

- lifecycle
- provenance
- relationships
- reconstruction
- historical evolution

The Corpus does not perform research.

The Corpus does not generate observations.

Those responsibilities belong to the Laboratory and Scientific Instruments.

---

## Guiding Principle

Scientific knowledge evolves through Scientific Objects.

The Corpus preserves and governs that evolution.
