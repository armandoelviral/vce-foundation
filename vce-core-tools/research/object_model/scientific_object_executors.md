# Scientific Object Executors (Candidate)

## Purpose

Executors perform approved Operations on Scientific Objects.

Executors do not define what is valid.

Governance determines admissibility.

Executors perform authorized action.

---

## Candidate Executor Types

### EX-001

Human Researcher

A human actor authorized to perform scientific operations.

---

### EX-002

Research Committee

A collective governance actor authorized to approve or execute higher-order operations.

---

### EX-003

Scientific Instrument

A system that generates or modifies Scientific Objects through governed observation.

---

### EX-004

Runtime

A verifiable execution environment capable of producing or transforming Scientific Objects through computational processes.

---

### EX-005

Automated Policy Engine

An automated system that executes policy-approved operations under predefined constraints.

---

### EX-006

External Trusted System

An external system whose outputs may trigger governed operations when admitted by policy.

---

## Executor Requirements

Every Executor must provide:

- identity
- authorization scope
- provenance
- operation capability
- audit trail

---

## Principle

Execution is separated from governance.

Governance determines whether an operation is allowed.

Executors perform allowed operations.
