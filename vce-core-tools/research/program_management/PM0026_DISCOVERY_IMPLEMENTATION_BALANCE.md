# PM-0026

Discovery–Implementation Balance

Version

1.0

Status

Active

---

Purpose

Maintain equilibrium between architectural discovery and empirical validation.

---

Rule

For every newly accepted concept introduced into the program, at least two previously accepted concepts shall advance toward implementation, testing or operational validation.

---

Rationale

Knowledge matures through alternating cycles of discovery and validation.

Unchecked discovery increases speculative complexity.

Unchecked implementation limits innovation.

Balanced iteration maximizes long-term program quality.

---

Allocation

Discovery

20%

Implementation

80%

---

Compliance

Architecture reviews shall verify that discovery does not significantly outpace implementation.
