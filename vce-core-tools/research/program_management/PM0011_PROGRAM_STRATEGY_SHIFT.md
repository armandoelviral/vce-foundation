# PM-0011

Program Strategy Shift

Version

1.0

Status

Active

---

# Decision

The Scientific Product Program transitions from architecture-driven discovery to implementation-driven discovery.

---

## Previous Allocation

Research

70%

Implementation

30%

---

## New Allocation

Implementation

80%

Research

20%

---

## Rationale

The program has reached sufficient methodological and architectural maturity.

Future architectural evolution shall primarily originate from implementation evidence rather than speculative expansion.

---

## Principle

Software becomes the principal scientific instrument of the program.

Implementation becomes the principal producer of architectural evidence.

Research remains responsible for interpreting and governing that evidence.

---

## Success Criterion

Every implementation cycle shall produce at least one measurable architectural, methodological or capability insight.

---

## Expected Outcome

The evolution of the program becomes increasingly evidence-driven while preserving methodological rigor.
