# Discovery Freeze

Version

1.0

Status

Active

---

Purpose

Protect implementation cycles from uncontrolled conceptual expansion.

---

Rule

During active implementation iterations:

New concepts shall be recorded.

New concepts shall not interrupt implementation unless:

- they invalidate an architectural invariant;
- they expose a critical safety issue;
- they invalidate a methodological assumption.

---

All remaining concepts enter the Research Backlog.
