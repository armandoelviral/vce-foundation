# Scientific Burden of Proof

Version: 1.0

Status: Active

---

Purpose

Define the evidentiary responsibility required before changing Scientific Products, Institutional Capabilities or Program Methodology.

---

Rule

The burden of proof belongs to the proposed change.

Existing architecture does not need to justify its existence continuously.

The proposed modification must demonstrate measurable improvement.

---

Evidence Required

Operational evidence

Architectural impact

Capability impact

Governance impact

Risk analysis

---

Promotion

Changes become permanent only after governed validation.
