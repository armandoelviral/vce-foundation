# PM-0002

Scientific Development Lifecycle

Version: 0.1

Status: Draft

---

# Purpose

Define the canonical lifecycle through which Scientific Products evolve from research questions into institutional capabilities.

---

# Lifecycle

Research Question

↓

Hypothesis

↓

Architecture

↓

Specification

↓

Canonical Contracts

↓

Reference Implementation

↓

Operational Validation

↓

Evidence Review

↓

Capability Promotion

↓

Institutional Learning

↓

Next Research Question

---

# Principle

Every implementation exists to validate or falsify architectural knowledge.

Software is evidence-producing infrastructure.

---

# Exit Criterion

Each completed lifecycle shall produce at least one of:

- promoted knowledge;
- rejected hypothesis;
- refined architecture;
- new capability;
- new research question.
