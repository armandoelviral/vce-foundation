# Formal Theorems

## Purpose

This registry contains formally demonstrated theorems produced by the VCE Research Program.

A theorem may only enter this registry after satisfying the formal admission criteria.

---

# Admission Criteria

A theorem shall only be admitted when all of the following conditions are satisfied:

1. The statement is formally specified.

2. A complete proof exists.

3. The proof has been independently reviewed.

4. The assumptions are explicitly documented.

5. The scope and limitations are formally stated.

---
