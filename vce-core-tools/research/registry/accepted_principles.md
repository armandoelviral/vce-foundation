# Accepted Principles

## Purpose

This registry contains principles that have demonstrated sustained operational validity within the VCE Research Program.

Accepted Principles are not declarations.

They are principles that have emerged through repeated application, experimental support or architectural necessity.

---

# AP-001

## Name

Evidence Before Theory

### Statement

Scientific theories shall emerge from accumulated evidence rather than precede it.

### Operational Evidence

- RC-0001
- EXP-0002
- Dependency Analysis
- Architectural Decisions

### Status

Accepted

# AP-002

## Name

Reconstructability

### Statement

Every significant scientific conclusion shall be traceable to its supporting observations, experiments, evidence and decisions.

### Operational Evidence

Entire Research Laboratory Architecture

### Status

Accepted

# AP-003

## Name

Falsification Before Promotion

### Statement

No scientific statement shall be promoted to higher epistemic status without surviving explicit attempts at falsification.
