## RQ-014

### Title

Necessary Conditions for Knowledge Continuity

---

### Research Question

What are the necessary conditions that allow the continuity of knowledge to survive across time?

More specifically,

is reconstructability alone sufficient,

or does continuity require additional independent conditions such as:

- evidence integrity;
- semantic preservation;
- contextual preservation;
- observer capability;
- intergenerational adoption;
- or other conditions yet to be identified?

Execution of the first logical counterexamples demonstrated that reconstructability alone does not completely explain knowledge continuity.

Instead, multiple independent candidate conditions began to emerge.

The objective of this research question is therefore no longer to evaluate reconstructability itself.

The objective is to identify the minimal architecture of conditions necessary for long-term knowledge continuity.

## RQ-015

### Title

Intergenerational Adoption and Knowledge Continuity

---

### Research Question

Is intergenerational adoption a necessary condition for knowledge continuity, or does it merely reflect continuity already achieved?

---

### Origin

EXP-0002

Counterexample:

CE-006

---

### Motivation

The execution of CE-006 demonstrated that a system may preserve:

- complete history;
- evidence integrity;
- language;
- contextual information;
- semantic mappings;
- qualified observers;

while nevertheless being intentionally abandoned by future generations.

This raises the possibility that continuity depends not only upon preservation and reconstructability, but also upon an explicit decision to continue.

Whether adoption is itself a necessary condition, or merely a consequence of continuity, remains unknown.
