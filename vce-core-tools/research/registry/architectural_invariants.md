# Architectural Invariants

Architectural Invariants describe properties that shall remain true throughout the evolution of the VCE Research Program.

Unlike implementation decisions, these invariants constrain the long-term identity of the program.

---

# AI-001

## No Mechanism Is Exempt

Every mechanism of the Research Program shall itself be subject to evidence, governance, audit, reconstruction and revision.

No protocol, theory, principle or architectural decision possesses permanent immunity.

Status

Candidate.

---

# AI-002

## Scientific Objects Improve The Program

Scientific Objects are not the terminal products of the Research Program.

They generate evidence capable of improving the mechanisms responsible for producing future Scientific Objects.

Status

Candidate.

---

# AI-003

## Methodological Evolution Precedes Theoretical Expansion

Whenever evidence reveals a weakness in the Research Program, preference shall be given to improving methodological mechanisms before introducing additional theoretical constructs.

Status

Candidate.

---

# AI-004

## Preserve Conceptual Identity

Architectural evolution shall preserve the conceptual identity of the Research Program while allowing continuous improvement of its mechanisms.

Status

Candidate.

---

# AI-005

## Minimal Explanatory Architecture

Among competing architectures capable of explaining the same phenomena, preference shall be given to the smallest architecture preserving explanatory power.

Status

Candidate.
