# Research Observations Registry

Observations (OBS) record empirical or methodological findings identified during the evolution of the VCE Research Program.

An Observation is not:

- a Design Hypothesis;
- a Scientific Hypothesis;
- an Accepted Principle;
- Constitutional Knowledge.

An Observation simply documents something judged sufficiently relevant to preserve for future investigation.

Observations may later evolve into:

Research Questions

↓

Design Hypotheses

↓

Scientific Hypotheses

if supported by subsequent evidence.

---

# OBS-001

## Title

The Research Program Became Its First Experimental Subject

### Context

During the construction of the VCE Research Program, the program itself became the first observable instance of the phenomena it seeks to investigate.

### Observation

The first successful application of the proposed research methodology occurred on the research program itself.

The co-evolution of governance, hypotheses, evidence, criticism and reconstruction became an empirical case study rather than merely a theoretical proposal.

### Possible Implications

- self-hosting scientific methodology
- recursive research programs
- methodological validation
- reconstructability

### Status

Observation only.

Further investigation required.

# OBS-002

## Title

Observation Layer Separation

### Context

While designing the Observatory it became evident that sufficiently mature systems separate execution from observation.

### Observation

The layer responsible for observing system evolution appears conceptually distinct from the layer responsible for executing system transitions.

This distinction emerged independently in both:

- Runtime Architecture
- Research Program Architecture

### Possible Implications

- observability architecture
- governance
- replay
- long-term reconstruction

### Status

Observation only.

Requires validation across additional domains.

# OBS-003

## Title

Falsification Criteria Should Precede Evidence Acquisition

### Context

During RC-0001 it became apparent that falsification criteria should be declared before literature review begins.

### Observation

Defining falsification criteria before consulting evidence reduces retrospective reinterpretation and confirmation bias.

The falsification specification itself becomes part of the permanent scientific record.

### Possible Implications

- research governance
- reproducibility
- methodological transparency

### Status

Observation only.

Requires comparison with existing philosophy of science and scientific methodology.

# OBS-004

## Title

Evidence Acquisition Has Independent Provenance

### Context

While defining the Evidence Acquisition workflow, three distinct provenance layers emerged.

### Observation

Scientific reconstructability appears to depend upon preserving:

Search Provenance

↓

Evidence Provenance

↓

Knowledge Provenance

These three provenance chains describe different stages of scientific reasoning and should not be conflated.

### Possible Implications

- provenance models
- scientific reproducibility
- research governance
- auditability

### Status

Observation only.

Requires validation through comparative literature review.
