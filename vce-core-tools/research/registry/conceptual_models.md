# Conceptual Models

## Purpose

This registry records conceptual models emerging from Research Cycles.

Conceptual Models are explanatory structures.

They are not yet accepted theories.

They remain subject to revision, falsification and refinement.

---

# CM-001

## Name

Knowledge Continuity Architecture

### Origin

RC-0001

### Description

Candidate explanatory model describing the conditions under which knowledge continuity may be preserved across time.

Current candidate components include:

- Historical Reconstruction
- Evidence Integrity
- Context Preservation
- Semantic Preservation
- Observer Capability
- Intergenerational Adoption

The model remains under active investigation.

Evidence
