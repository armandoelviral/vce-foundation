# Constitutional Decisions Registry

## CD-001 — Explicit Epistemic Status

Status: Adopted

Decision: Every program artifact shall declare its epistemic status.

---

## CD-002 — Falsification Before Formalization

Status: Adopted

Decision: Every new hypothesis shall pass a Falsification Gate before formalization.

---

## CD-003 — Evidence Before Expansion

Status: Adopted

Decision: No new theoretical line shall be opened while active hypotheses lack an explicit evidence-generation plan.
