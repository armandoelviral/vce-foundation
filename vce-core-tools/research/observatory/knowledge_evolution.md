# Knowledge Evolution

The Observatory records how knowledge evolves across time.

Knowledge is never treated as static.

Instead, every epistemic artifact progresses through explicit transitions.

---

## Evolution Path

Observation (OBS)

↓

Research Question (RQ)

↓

Design Hypothesis (DH)

↓

Scientific Hypothesis (H)

↓

Conceptual Model (M)

↓

Formalization (F)

↓

Evidence (E)

↓

Accepted Principle (P)

↓

Constitutional Knowledge (C)

---

## Governing Principles

Knowledge evolves through:

- criticism
- evidence
- revision
- reconstruction

Authority never precedes evidence.

Evidence enables reconstruction.

Reconstruction enables audit.

Audit enables adoption.

Adoption preserves continuity.

Continuity enables future understanding.

---

## Observatory Objective

Observe how knowledge changes without losing the provenance that explains why those changes occurred.
