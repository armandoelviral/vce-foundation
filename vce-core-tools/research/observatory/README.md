# VCE Observatory

The Observatory is the long-term observational layer of the VCE Research Program.

Its objective is not to evaluate researchers.

Its objective is to observe, reconstruct and preserve the evolution of scientific knowledge.

Unlike dashboards that monitor productivity, the Observatory monitors epistemic evolution.

The Observatory continuously observes:

- Research Cycles
- Research Questions
- Design Hypotheses
- Scientific Hypotheses
- Evidence
- Formalizations
- Counterexamples
- Accepted Principles
- Constitutional Knowledge

The Observatory serves as the historical memory of the research program.

Its outputs enable future generations to reconstruct not only what the program concluded, but also how those conclusions evolved through evidence, criticism and revision.

The Observatory itself remains subject to continuous methodological review.
