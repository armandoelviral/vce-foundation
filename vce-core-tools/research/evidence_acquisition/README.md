# Evidence Acquisition

## Purpose

Evidence Acquisition governs how external scientific sources are identified before entering the Knowledge Integration Pipeline.

The objective is to ensure that literature collection remains systematic, reproducible and auditable.

Evidence acquisition precedes literature interpretation.

Searching and interpreting are intentionally treated as separate scientific activities.

---

# Guiding Principles

Evidence shall be acquired through explicit search strategies.

Searches shall be reproducible.

Negative evidence shall be actively sought.

Search history shall remain reconstructable.
