# Evidence Acquisition Protocol

Every literature acquisition follows the same sequence.

---

## Step 1

Define Research Question.

---

## Step 2

Define Falsification Criteria.

---

## Step 3

Generate Search Terms.

Direct terms.

Synonyms.

Adjacent disciplines.

Negative searches.

---

## Step 4

Record Search Strategy.

Every search shall remain reproducible.

---

## Step 5

Register Sources.

Every retrieved source receives a unique identifier.

No interpretation occurs during acquisition.

---

## Step 6

Transfer Sources to Evidence Cards.

Only then may Comparative Review begin.
