# Novelty Matrix

A hypothesis may conclude one of five outcomes.

---

## A

Already established.

Reject novelty.

---

## B

Known concept under different terminology.

Rename accordingly.

---

## C

Partial overlap.

Clearly delimit contribution.

---

## D

Novel integration.

Individual components already exist.

The contribution lies in their integration.

---

## E

Potentially novel.

No equivalent found.

Requires external review.
