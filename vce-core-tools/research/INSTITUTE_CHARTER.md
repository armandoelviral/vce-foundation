# VCE Research Institute Charter

Version

0.1

Status

Foundational Charter

---

# Purpose

The VCE Research Institute exists to investigate,
design, validate and institutionalize reproducible
knowledge systems.

Its objective is not merely to produce software.

Its objective is to produce Scientific Products,
Human-AI Standards, governance models and
institutional knowledge capable of surviving
independent validation.

---

# Mission

To transform research questions into
institutional knowledge through reproducible
evidence.

---

# Vision

A future in which organizations can reproduce
not only software, but also:

- knowledge;
- organizational capabilities;
- scientific laboratories;
- research methodologies;
- institutional learning.

---

# Scope

The Institute studies systems capable of
producing, validating, governing and evolving
knowledge.

Its scope includes, but is not limited to:

- Runtime Engineering;
- Scientific Product Engineering;
- Knowledge Engineering;
- Laboratory Engineering;
- Human-AI Standards;
- Organizational Capability Engineering.

---

# Research Philosophy

The Institute does not promote ideas.

The Institute creates the conditions under
which ideas can survive.

Evidence determines promotion.

Scientific Destruction determines resilience.

Independent validation determines permanence.

---

# Knowledge Governance

Knowledge evolves through explicit maturity
levels.

Research Question

↓

Observation

↓

Hypothesis

↓

Candidate Foundational Statement

↓

Candidate Principle

↓

Principle

↓

Institutional Standard

Promotion requires evidence.

Demotion remains possible.

---

# Scientific Method

Every Scientific Product is expected to:

- formulate explicit questions;

- accumulate reproducible evidence;

- survive Scientific Destruction;

- preserve institutional memory;

- improve future investigations.

---

# Institutional Memory

The Institute maintains version-controlled
institutional memory.

Knowledge must progressively become
independent from individual participants.

Institutional memory constitutes one of the
primary research assets of the Institute.

---

# Reproducibility

Every important artifact should be capable
of independent reproduction.

The Institute considers reproducibility a
necessary condition for scientific maturity.

---

# Continuous Evolution

The Institute recognizes that:

methodologies evolve;

architectures evolve;

Scientific Products evolve;

the Institute itself evolves.

No artifact is exempt from revision.

---

# Long-Term Objective

To understand how reproducible systems can
generate reproducible knowledge,
institutional capabilities,
reproducible laboratories,
and eventually reproducible research institutes.

---

# Guiding Principle

The Institute does not seek certainty.

The Institute seeks increasingly reliable
methods for discovering, validating and
institutionalizing knowledge.
