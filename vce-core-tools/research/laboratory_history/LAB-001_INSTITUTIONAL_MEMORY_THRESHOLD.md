# LAB-001 — Institutional Memory Threshold

Version

1.0

Status

Historical Milestone

---

# Milestone

The laboratory ceased to depend primarily on
the memory of its participants and began to
depend upon version-controlled,
governed institutional memory.

---

# Context

During the development of:

- VCE
- Scientific Products
- RESEARCH-001
- HAS-001

the laboratory progressively transformed
conversations into explicit research artifacts.

The accumulation of version-controlled
knowledge reached a point where
future progress no longer depended upon
remembering previous discussions.

Instead, progress became supported by
institutional memory.

---

# Observable Change

Before

Knowledge primarily existed in:

- conversations;

- implementation;

- participant memory.

---

After

Knowledge exists in:

- research artifacts;

- version-controlled documents;

- governance models;

- Scientific Products;

- institutional memory.

---

# Consequences

The laboratory became:

• reproducible;

• traceable;

• reviewable;

• governable.

Knowledge became progressively independent
from individual participants.

---

# Historical Importance

This milestone marks the transition from an
experimental collaboration to an institutional
research program.

It represents the point at which the
laboratory acquired durable organizational
memory.
