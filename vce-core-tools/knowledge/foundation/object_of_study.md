# Object of Study

VCE studies verifiable continuity.

More precisely:

the continuity between original intent and future understanding.

The project does not primarily study storage, archives, databases, or logs.

It studies how intent, context, evidence, policy, provenance, replay, reconstruction, interpretation, and understanding can remain connected across time.
