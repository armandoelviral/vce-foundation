# VCE Principles #72–#96

## #72 Research Independence
First products finance research without compromising scientific direction.

## #73 Invisible Research
Customer-facing features solve customer problems. Research instrumentation remains invisible unless it creates direct customer value.

## #74 Reconstructability First
Evidence has no value unless it enables decision reconstruction.

## #75 Customers Buy Answers
Customers do not buy cryptography. Customers buy the ability to answer the next difficult question.

## #76 Answerability
Evidence fulfills its purpose only when it enables a future answer to an important question.

## #77 Questions Drive Architecture
Questions define the architecture, not artifacts.

## #78 Operational Validation
Research must be validated through operational reality, not presentation.

## #79 Product–Research Flywheel
Products validate research. Research improves products.

## #80 Research Platform
VCE is not a product. VCE exists to investigate, validate, and evolve architectures for verifiable knowledge continuity.

## #81 Commercial Validation
Commercialization validates research under real-world conditions. Revenue is evidence of usefulness, not scientific truth.

## #82 Scientific Independence
Revenue may accelerate research. Revenue must never determine scientific conclusions.

## #83 Reconstructability over Preservation
The objective is not to preserve artifacts. The objective is to preserve the ability to reconstruct their meaning.

## #84 Observable Before Abstract
Every new phase must begin with an observable problem, not an attractive abstraction.

## #85 One Problem at a Time
Every commercial product must solve one clearly defined problem exceptionally well before expanding scope.

## #86 Falsifiable Progress
Every research hypothesis must define how it can be disproven.

## #87 Separation of Missions
VCE advances scientific knowledge. Commercial products deliver customer value.

## #88 Reconstructability is the Measure
The value of a system is measured not by how much it stores, but by how faithfully it enables future reconstruction.

## #89 Multidimensional Rigor
Scientific, engineering, commercial, operational, and epistemological rigor must evolve together.

## #90 Intent Preservation
Information without intent becomes ambiguity. Intent without information becomes speculation.

## #91 Continuity Chain
Knowledge continuity depends on preserving relationships between intent, context, evidence, and interpretation.

## #92 Continuity of Meaning
The objective is to preserve the continuity between original intent and future understanding through verifiable evidence.

## #93 Meaning Emerges Through Continuity
Meaning emerges from verifiable continuity between intent, context, evidence, and interpretation.

## #94 Language Before Code
A stable language precedes stable architecture. Code changes. Language defines continuity.

## #95 Knowledge Must Exist Outside the Code
Code implements. Documents preserve intent.

## #96 Research-First Repository
A research repository should reflect the lifecycle of knowledge, not only the layout of source code.
