# Foundational Axiom I

La continuidad es el mecanismo.

La esencia es el criterio.

La comprensión es el propósito.

Preservar la intención para hacer posible la comprensión futura.

------------------------------------

# VCE Ontology

## Purpose

This ontology defines the fundamental entities studied by the Verifiable Compute Engine (VCE).

It intentionally precedes architecture, software implementation and products.

Its objective is to establish a stable conceptual language capable of surviving technological change.

---

# Ontological Principle

VCE does not primarily study software.

VCE studies how the continuity between original intent and future understanding can be preserved through verifiable evidence.

Every architectural component exists only insofar as it contributes to preserving that continuity.

---

# The Continuity Chain

```
Intent
    ↓
Decision
    ↓
Execution
    ↓
Evidence
    ↓
Context
    ↓
Policy
    ↓
Provenance
    ↓
Verification
    ↓
Replay
    ↓
Reconstruction
    ↓
Interpretation
    ↓
Understanding
```

The chain represents the progressive transformation of human intention into future understanding.

Each entity exists because it preserves part of this transition.

---

# Entity Definitions

## Question

A request for understanding.

Questions initiate investigation.

Without questions there is no reconstruction.

---

## Intent

The original purpose that motivates an action or decision.

Intent precedes every decision.

Intent is never directly observable.

It must be inferred through evidence.

---

## Decision

A deliberate selection among possible alternatives.

A decision operationalizes intent.

Every decision should be reconstructable.

---

## Execution

The observable realization of a decision.

Execution transforms decisions into events.

Execution generates evidence.

---

## Evidence

Observable information generated during execution.

Evidence records what occurred.

Evidence alone does not preserve meaning.

---

## Context

The surrounding conditions necessary to correctly interpret evidence.

Context includes temporal, organizational, technical and semantic relationships.

Context prevents evidence from becoming ambiguity.

---

## Policy

The explicit rules governing acceptable decisions.

Policies constrain execution.

Policies explain why a decision was considered valid.

---

## Provenance

The verifiable history of how evidence, context and decisions originated and evolved.

Provenance preserves traceability.

---

## Verification

The process of establishing confidence that preserved relationships remain authentic and unmodified.

Verification establishes trust.

It does not establish truth.

---

## Replay

Deterministic reconstruction of historical execution.

Replay validates reconstructability.

Replay is not simulation.

Replay attempts to reproduce execution under preserved conditions.

---

## Reconstruction

The process of recovering sufficient understanding of a historical decision.

Reconstruction integrates evidence, context, policy and provenance.

---

## Interpretation

Human or machine understanding produced from successful reconstruction.

Interpretation may evolve.

Interpretation is not permanently fixed.

---

## Understanding

The highest-level outcome of the continuity chain.

Understanding represents the successful preservation of meaning across time.

Understanding is the objective.

Not storage.

---

# Emergent Properties

The following concepts are not stored directly.

They emerge from successful preservation of the continuity chain.

* Answerability
* Accountability
* Explainability
* Trust
* Institutional Memory
* Historical Continuity

---

# Architectural Consequence

Every future component added to VCE must answer one question:

> Which part of the continuity chain does this protect?

If no clear answer exists, the component probably does not belong to the core architecture.

---

# Scope Boundary

VCE does not attempt to preserve absolute truth.

VCE does not reconstruct information that never existed.

VCE does not eliminate uncertainty.

VCE investigates architectures that maximize reconstructability given the available evidence.

---

# Central Ontological Statement

The fundamental object of study of VCE is neither software nor data.

It is the verifiable continuity between original intent and future understanding.
