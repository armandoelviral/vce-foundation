## Purpose

Define the canonical behavioral contract shared by every implementation of the Scientific Product.

Implementations may differ.

Behavior shall not.

---

## Required Capabilities

The system shall:

Accept Objectives.

Create Cases.

Generate Recommendations.

Capture Expert Decisions.

Capture Expert Rationale.

Capture Operational Evidence.

Evaluate Candidate Capabilities.

Support Governance Decisions.

Create Institutional Capabilities.

Maintain Institutional Memory.

Produce complete Audit Trails.

---

## Forbidden Behavior

No recommendation may become policy without governance.

No capability may exist without evidence.

No expert decision may exist without provenance.

No evidence may lose traceability.

No implementation may bypass canonical contracts.

---

## Success Criterion

Two independent implementations shall produce equivalent behavioral outcomes when executed with equivalent inputs.
