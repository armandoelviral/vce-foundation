# H-PM-002 — Scientific Destruction

Status:
Experimental

Category:
Methodological Hypothesis

---

## Definition

Scientific Destruction is the deliberate and systematic process of attempting to refute a hypothesis, pattern, architecture, or principle before promoting it within the program.

Scientific Destruction does not destroy knowledge.

It destroys insufficiently supported knowledge.

---

## Motivation

The greatest architectural risk is not implementing a bad idea.

It is institutionalizing an insufficiently challenged one.

Scientific Destruction intentionally exposes ideas to criticism before they become part of the methodology.

---

## Expected Outcome

Ideas that survive Scientific Destruction become stronger.

Ideas that fail Scientific Destruction prevent future technical debt.

---

## Scientific Question

Does deliberate falsification improve long-term architectural quality?
