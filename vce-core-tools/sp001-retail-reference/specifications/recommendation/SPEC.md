# Recommendation Service

Version: 0.1

Status: Draft

Classification: Specification

---

## Purpose

Produce explainable recommendations that assist experts in achieving explicit organizational objectives.

---

## Inputs

Context

Policies

Evidence

Capabilities

Objectives

---

## Outputs

Recommendation

Confidence

Explanation

Simulation Links

---

## Responsibilities

Generate recommendations.

Maintain recommendation history.

Support versioning.

Never execute recommendations.

Never institutionalize recommendations.

---

## Invariants

Recommendations are immutable after publication.

Recommendations are versioned.

Recommendations are explainable.

Recommendations are attributable.

Recommendations never bypass Human Expertise.

---

## Success Criteria

Recommendation generated.

Explanation generated.

Confidence calculated.

Audit trail preserved.

---

## Failure Conditions

Missing context.

Conflicting policies.

Insufficient evidence.

Invalid objective.
