# EPIC085-D1 Policy Contract

## Goal

Define governance policies as verifiable artifacts.

## Core Principle

Evidence admission decisions must be traceable to an approved policy.

## Required Properties

Policies must be:

- versioned
- approved
- attestable
- revocable
- replayable

## Policy Scope

Policies may define:

- CPS thresholds
- process catalog requirements
- operational context requirements
- governance requirements
- witness consensus requirements

## Required Outputs

A policy must expose:

- policy_id
- policy_version
- policy_hash

## Non Goals

Policies do not execute business logic.

Policies govern evidence admission.
