# Principle #5: Democratization of Algorithmic Control

## Statement

Every computational decision capable of affecting people must remain:

- Auditable
- Attributable
- Governable
- Explainable
- Accountable

through verifiable computational citizenship.

## Rationale

Automation increases decision-making capacity.

Citizenship increases accountability.

Sustainable autonomous systems require both.

## Core Principle

Automation scales decisions.

Computational citizenship scales responsibility.

## Constitutional Requirement

No computational claim may exist without:

- Provenance
- Identity
- Governance
- Response Validity

## Objective

Ensure that autonomous agents remain accountable to human institutions through verifiable governance mechanisms.

## Relationship to Previous Principles

Verifiable Execution
        ↓

Verifiable Provenance
        ↓

Computational Citizenship
        ↓

Response Validity
        ↓

Democratization of Algorithmic Control

## Long-Term Vision

Every computational decision capable of impacting a person should remain reconstructible, auditable, and attributable to accountable computational citizens.

## Canonical Rule

No Algorithmic Authority Without Accountability.
