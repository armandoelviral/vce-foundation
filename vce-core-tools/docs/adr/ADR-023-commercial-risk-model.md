# ADR-023

Title:
Commercial Risk Model

Status:
Accepted

Date:
YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

The VCE platform introduces:

- Verifiable governance
- Constitutional controls
- Reputation systems
- Federated consensus

These capabilities introduce commercial,
operational, and regulatory risks.

## Decision

The platform shall define explicit trust
boundaries for:

1. Liability
2. Network Admission
3. Jurisdictional Sovereignty

## Risk Categories

### Liability Risk

Verification does not imply liability transfer.

No customer loss creates unlimited liability.

### Network Admission Risk

No node may participate without
constitutional admission.

### Regulatory Drift Risk

Evidence must obey jurisdiction.

## Consequences

Commercial deployment requires:

- MSA controls
- Admission controls
- Jurisdiction controls

before production rollout.
