# VCE Research Program

## Purpose

This document defines how knowledge is created, validated, preserved and incorporated into the Verifiable Civilization Engine (VCE).

It governs every research activity performed within the program.

No research result becomes accepted knowledge without following this process.

---

# Scientific Purpose

The objective of VCE is not merely to produce knowledge.

Its objective is to produce knowledge that remains understandable, reproducible and improvable across generations.

---

# Research Question

The central research question of VCE is:

> Under which conditions can knowledge preserve its continuity through verifiable evidence, independently of technologies, organizations and generations?

Every research line shall contribute to answering part of this question.

---

# Object of Study

The object of study is:

> The verifiable continuity between original intent and future understanding.

Everything produced by VCE shall relate explicitly to this object of study.

# Research Cycles

Every advancement is registered as a Research Cycle (RC).

Each RC shall contain:

• Research Question

• Motivation

• Object of Study

• Hypothesis

• Formal Model

• Experiment

• Evidence

• Results

• Remaining Uncertainty

• Impact on the Compendium

---

# Acceptance Criteria

A proposal becomes accepted only after satisfying progressively:

Internal Consistency

↓

Compatibility with Accepted Principles

↓

Formalization

↓

Executable Specification

↓

Experimental Validation

↓

Independent Verification

↓

Scientific Review

↓

Compendium Integration

Acceptance is always provisional.

Every accepted contribution remains subject to future evidence.

---

# Negative Results

Negative results are considered valuable scientific outcomes.

Every failed experiment shall be preserved if it contributes to understanding the limits of the theory.

Failure is evidence.

Loss of traceability is not.

---

# Relationship with Products

Products are experimental manifestations of the research program.

Every product must explicitly identify:

• Which hypothesis it validates.

• Which authority it governs.

• Which evidence it produces.

• Which continuity it preserves.

Products do not define the theory.

They validate it.

---

# Relationship with the Compendium

The Compendium is the canonical repository of accepted knowledge.

Research precedes the Compendium.

The Compendium never precedes research.

---

# Long-Term Objective

The long-term objective of the VCE Research Program is to establish a reproducible scientific discipline capable of preserving the continuity of knowledge through verifiable evidence, formal reasoning and executable implementations.
