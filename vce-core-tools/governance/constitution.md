# Constitution

## Preamble

Knowledge survives civilizations only when its continuity survives those who created it.

The purpose of this Constitution is to preserve that continuity through evidence, scientific discipline and verifiable evolution, so that future generations may understand, reconstruct and continue the work without depending on its original authors.

It governs the evolution of the research program independently of technologies, implementations or contributors.

No implementation supersedes the Constitution.

No product supersedes the research program.

No individual supersedes the evidence.

---

# Article I

## Mission

The mission of VCE is:

> Preserve original intent to enable future understanding.

Every decision made within the program shall preserve this mission.

---

# Article II

## Object of Study

The object of study of VCE is:

> The verifiable continuity between original intent and future understanding.

Every theory, runtime, product and experiment shall contribute to investigating, validating or preserving some aspect of this continuity.

---

# Article III

## Scientific Identity

VCE is simultaneously:

- a scientific research program;
- a knowledge compendium;
- a formal theory under development;
- a runtime ecosystem;
- a collection of verifiable artifacts.

These dimensions are inseparable.

---

# Article IV

## Categories of Knowledge

Every contribution belongs to exactly one category.

Accepted Principle

Model

Hypothesis

Evidence

Formal Theorem

Verifiable Artifact

Future Work

Categories shall never be mixed.

---

# Article V

## Knowledge Maturity

Knowledge evolves through explicit stages.

Question

↓

Hypothesis

↓

Model

↓

Formalization

↓

Implementation

↓

Experiment

↓

Evidence

↓

Accepted Principle

↓

Constitutional Knowledge

No stage may be skipped.

---

# Article VI

## Scientific Method

Ideas are not accepted because they appear convincing.

Ideas become accepted only after surviving evidence.

Scientific humility takes precedence over speculative certainty.

---

# Article VII

## Evidence

Authority emerges from evidence.

No actor, implementation, product or document possesses intrinsic authority.

Authority must always be justified by reproducible evidence.

---

# Article VIII

## Continuity

Continuity is the mechanism.

Essence is the criterion.

Understanding is the purpose.

Every modification shall preserve continuity unless an explicit constitutional revision is approved.

---

# Article IX

## Traceability

Every accepted contribution shall preserve:

origin

motivation

relationships

evidence

revision history

Traceability constitutes part of the scientific evidence.

---

# Article X

## Products

Products are not independent goals.

They are experimental specializations derived from the common theoretical core.

Every product shall explicitly document:

- the hypothesis it validates;
- the authority it governs;
- the evidence it produces;
- the continuity it preserves.

---

# Article XI

## Research Ledger

Every significant advancement shall generate a Research Cycle.

Every Research Cycle shall record:

Question

Hypothesis

Experiment

Evidence

Decision

Impact

Updated Compendium

---

# Article XII

## Constitutional Revision

Constitutional knowledge shall evolve only through explicit revision.

Every revision must preserve complete historical traceability.

Nothing shall be removed.

Knowledge evolves by extension rather than erasure.

---

# Closing Statement

The purpose of VCE is not merely to preserve conclusions.

Its purpose is to preserve the conditions under which conclusions may continue to be reconstructed, verified, criticized and improved.

The continuity of the program takes precedence over the permanence of any individual implementation, contributor or technology.
