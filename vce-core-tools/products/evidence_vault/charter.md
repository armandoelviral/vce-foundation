# Evidence Vault Charter

## Product

Evidence Vault

## Mission

Make every critical AI decision answerable.

## Category

Decision Reconstruction Infrastructure.

## Purpose

Evidence Vault helps organizations reconstruct, justify, and verify critical AI decisions long after they were made.

## Relationship to VCE

Evidence Vault is the first commercial implementation built on top of the VCE research platform.

VCE advances the research.

Evidence Vault validates the research under real operating conditions.
