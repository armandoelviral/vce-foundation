# RC-001 Constitution Freeze

Repository Constitution
Specification Freeze

Version

1.0

Status

Frozen

Target

RC-001 Version 0.4

Target Model

Freeze Candidate

---

## Purpose

Define the normative
freeze boundary
for RC-001
Repository Constitution
Version 0.4.

This Freeze
does not redefine
the Constitution.

It identifies
which constitutional
semantics
are eligible
to become
stable normative
authority.

---

## Freeze Basis

RC-001 Version 0.4
has undergone:

Constitution Refutation
Cycle 1.

Adversarial Authority
Refutation Cycle 2.

Targeted Authority
Refutation Cycle 3.

The Constitution
was reduced
from an architecture-heavy
and immutable model

to a minimal,
versioned,
evidence-driven
authority model.

The current candidate
contains no
domain-specific
or architecture-specific
runtime semantics.

---

## Frozen Purpose

The Constitution
shall define
the highest
repository-level
normative authority

within an identified
Repository Authority Context.

The Constitution
shall remain minimal.

It shall govern
authority and evolution,

not implementation
or domain semantics.

---

## Frozen Authority Context

Constitutional authority
shall exist
within an explicitly
identified
Repository Authority Context.

Authority
shall not silently extend
to:

Forks.

Unrelated repositories.

External systems.

Independent authority
lineages.

Authority Context transitions
shall remain
explicit
and traceable.

---

## Frozen Highest Authority

Within one
Repository Authority Context,

the currently authoritative
Constitutional version
shall represent
the highest
repository normative
authority.

Lower normative
artifacts
shall remain
subordinate
to that authority.

---

## Frozen Current Authority Semantics

Current constitutional
authority
shall resolve
unambiguously.

Authority
shall not be inferred
from:

File presence.

Version recency.

Commit order.

Implementation behavior.

Test behavior.

Historical popularity.

Current authority
shall remain explicit.

---

## Frozen Constitutional Principle Authority

Constitutional Principles
shall require
explicit authority.

Existence
within the repository
shall not create
constitutional authority.

Constitutional Principles
shall remain distinct
from Architecture Principles.

---

## Frozen Minimality

The Constitution
shall remain minimal.

A concept
shall not enter
constitutional authority
solely because
it is:

Architecturally important.

Commercially important.

Historically important.

Widely implemented.

Technically successful.

Constitutional promotion
shall require
independent justification.

---

## Frozen Conflict Resolution

When lower-level
normative authority
conflicts
with the currently
authoritative Constitution

within the same
Repository Authority Context,

the Constitution
shall prevail.

Conflict resolution
shall not permit
silent reinterpretation.

If constitutional semantics
are wrong,

they shall evolve
through explicit revision.

---

## Frozen Versioned Authority

The Constitution
shall be versioned.

Historical versions
may coexist.

Published
Constitutional baselines
shall remain
historically stable.

Future versions
may:

Clarify.

Narrow.

Extend.

Supersede.

Replace.

Invalidate.

earlier constitutional
semantics

through explicit
versioned evolution.

---

## Frozen Constitutional Evolution

Constitutional evolution
shall begin
through a traceable Trigger.

Constitutional revision
shall traverse
the applicable
Repository Specification
Lifecycle.

Constitutional evolution
shall require:

Investigation.

Canonical specification.

Normative review.

Refutation.

Promotion eligibility.

Explicit Promotion Gate.

Version authority transition.

Constitutional stability
shall not mean
constitutional immutability.

---

## Frozen Evidence Rule

Evidence
shall prevail
over attachment.

No constitutional principle
shall remain authoritative
solely because
it is:

Old.

Familiar.

Elegant.

Commercially valuable.

Historically important.

Widely implemented.

Evidence-driven
constitutional revision
shall remain possible.

---

## Frozen Historical Traceability

Authoritative
Constitutional baselines
shall remain
historically traceable.

Promotion decision.

Authority status.

Authority transition.

Supersession history.

Invalidation history.

Revision trigger.

Refutation evidence.

Compatibility impact.

Historical evidence

when authority changes.

## Frozen Authority Metadata Principle

Constitutional authority
shall remain explicit
and traceable.

Repository Authority Context.

Constitution version.

Authority status.

Promotion decision.

Effective authority transition.

Historical predecessor,

Database.

Git representation.

File format.

Serialization.

Storage system.

## Frozen Bootstrap Authority

The Constitution
shall not claim
to create
its own
original authority.

Initial constitutional
authority
shall require
an explicit
Bootstrap Authority event.

Bootstrap Authority
shall occur
at most once
per constitutional
authority lineage.

Bootstrap Authority
shall not be reused
to:

Replace.

Supersede.

Invalidate.

Bypass.

or otherwise
circumvent

existing
constitutional authority.

Subsequent authority
transitions
shall use
normal constitutional
promotion.

---
