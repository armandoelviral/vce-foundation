# ENGINEERING RULE 013

Title

Asset Verification

Status

Active

---

## Rule

Every engineering asset shall have at least
one executable contract before it may be
declared complete.

---

## Rationale

Engineering assets require objective and
repeatable evidence of correctness.

A document, model, rule, charter,
specification or implementation shall not be
considered complete solely because it exists.

Its required properties must be protected by
at least one executable contract.

---

## Consequence

Every engineering asset must identify an
associated executable contract.

An asset without an executable contract
remains incomplete.

Executable contracts must pass before the
asset may advance to COMPLETE or FROZEN
status.
