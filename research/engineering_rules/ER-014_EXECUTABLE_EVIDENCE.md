# ENGINEERING RULE 014

Title

Executable Evidence

Status

Active

---

## Rule

When an executable contract completely
demonstrates the state of an engineering
asset, no additional audit document shall
be created to duplicate that evidence.

---

## Rationale

Executable evidence is the primary source
of truth.

Documentation shall explain.

Executable contracts shall prove.

---

## Consequence

The PASS state of the executable contract
constitutes the audit evidence.
