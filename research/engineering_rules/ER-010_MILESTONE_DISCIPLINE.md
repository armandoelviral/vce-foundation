# ENGINEERING RULE 010

Title

Milestone Discipline

Status

Active

---

## Rule

No new milestone shall begin while the
current milestone contains unfinished
deliverables or unresolved architectural
changes.

---

## Rationale

Milestones are completed sequentially.

Architectural redesign shall be completed
before subsequent milestones begin.

---

## Consequence

A milestone is closed only after its
deliverables satisfy their Definition of
Done and its Closure Report has been
accepted.
