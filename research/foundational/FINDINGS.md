# Foundational Findings Register

Version

0.1

Status

Research

---

## F-001 — Trust Is Broader Than Security

Finding

Trust
shall not be
treated as
a synonym
for Security.

Security
is one
trust concern.

Other concerns
may include:

Identity.

Integrity.

Evidence.

Traceability.

Reproducibility.

Certification.

Applicability
depends upon
the domain
and trust profile.

---

## F-002 — Trust May Be Emergent

Finding

Current evidence
does not establish
Trust
as the most
fundamental abstraction.

Trust may emerge
from a more general
Claim Appraisal process.

Status

Provisional.

---

## F-003 — Appraisal / Decision Separation

Finding

Claim Assessment
and Domain Decision
represent
different responsibilities.

The Platform
may evaluate
support for a Claim.

The Domain
decides
what action
to take.

---

## F-004 — Truth Boundary

Finding

Claim appraisal
does not establish
objective truth.

SUPPORTED
means only
that declared Evidence
satisfies declared
Appraisal Criteria
for a declared Claim.

---

## F-005 — CTA Minimality

Finding

The value
of the Common Trust Architecture
shall depend
on its ability
to remain
small,
stable,
reusable,
and independent

while Domain Runtimes
evolve around it.

Platform success
shall not be measured
by the number
of abstractions
added to CTA.

---

## F-006 — CTA Is Not a Framework

Finding

The Common Trust Architecture
shall define
architectural semantics,

not:

Programming languages.

Class hierarchies.

SDKs.

Libraries.

Execution engines.

Cloud vendors.

Domain implementations.

---

## F-007 — Implementation Independence

Finding

Python,
OpenCV,
Rust,
CUDA,
ONNX,
WASM,
Docker,
and future technologies

shall remain
replaceable
implementation choices.

Normative semantics
must survive
their replacement.

---

## F-008 — Commercial Narrative Is Domain-Specific

Finding

Each Domain Runtime
requires
its own
commercial narrative.

Internal Platform
architecture
shall not become
the customer-facing
value proposition.

---

## F-009 — Research Is Applied

Finding

Research
shall not exist
for its own sake.

Commercial problems
shall drive
scientific investigation.

Scientific investigation
shall produce
reusable architectural
knowledge.

Reusable architecture
shall enable
commercial innovation.

---

## F-010 — Evidence Over Attachment

Finding

Architectural ideas
shall remain
replaceable.

Evidence
shall govern
promotion,
revision,
and rejection.

The objective
is not
to preserve
existing ideas.

The objective
is to build
something:

Solid.

Reproducible.

Defensible.

Durable.

Domain-independent
where evidence
supports that claim.
