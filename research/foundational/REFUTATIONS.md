# Foundational Refutation Register

Version

0.1

Status

Research

## R-001 — Replay Is Not Core Trust

Target Hypothesis

Replay belongs
to the minimal
Trust Kernel.

Refutation

Multiple domains
can establish
meaningful confidence
without requiring
historical replay.

Result

Refuted.

Classification

Replay
is a candidate
Trust Service.

---

## R-002 — Certification Is Not Core Trust

Target Hypothesis

Certification
is required
for all trust.

Refutation

A claim may be
supported by evidence
without external
certification.

Result

Refuted.

Classification

Certification
is a candidate
Trust Service.

Execution
is required

Static artifacts,
documents,
keys,

and other subjects
may be evaluated
without execution.

Refuted
as universal primitive.

## R-007 — Evidence Without Claim

Target Hypothesis

Evidence alone
can establish
trust.

Refutation

Evidence cannot be
meaningfully appraised
without knowing
what proposition
it is intended
to support
or refute.

Result

Refuted.

Claim remains
a primitive candidate.

---

## R-008 — Evidence Without Criteria

Target Hypothesis

Claim plus Evidence
is sufficient
for appraisal.

Refutation

The same Evidence
can yield
different outcomes
under different
evaluation criteria.

Result

Refuted.

Appraisal Criteria
remain a primitive
candidate.

## R-009 — Domain Decision in Core

Target Hypothesis

Trust Evaluation
should produce
a domain decision.

Refutation

Clinical,
financial,
legal,
visual,
and other domains
require independent
domain policies
and authority boundaries.

Result

Refuted.

Domain Decision
remains downstream.

---

## R-010 — Generic Trust Decision

Target Hypothesis

A universal
TRUSTED / UNTRUSTED
decision is required
between appraisal
and domain action.

Refutation

Domain policy
can consume
Claim Assessment
directly.

A generic trust boolean
adds ambiguity
and can imply
authority the Platform
does not possess.

Result

Provisionally Refuted.

## R-011 — Supported Equals True

Target Hypothesis

SUPPORTED
may be treated
as TRUE.

Refutation

Evidence can satisfy
declared criteria
without establishing
objective truth.

Result

Refuted.

SUPPORTED
shall remain
an evidentiary relation,
not an ontological
truth claim.

## R-012 — Not Supported Equals False

Target Hypothesis

NOT_SUPPORTED
may be treated
as FALSE.

Refutation

Lack of sufficient
evidence does not
prove falsity.

Result

Refuted.
