# Knowledge Registry Model

Version

1.0

Status

Draft

---

## Purpose

Define the normative registry model for all
Commerce Knowledge Objects.

The Registry is the single authoritative
repository of canonical commerce knowledge.

---

## Registered Object Types

Canonical Terms.

Semantic Relationships.

Normative Claims.

Business Rules.

Capabilities.

---

## Registry Responsibilities

Assign immutable identifiers.

Preserve canonical definitions.

Track lifecycle state.

Maintain semantic traceability.

Prevent duplicate concepts.

---

## Registry Properties

Canonical.

Deterministic.

Traceable.

Versioned.

Auditable.

Immutable Identity.

---

## Registry Lifecycle

Allocated.

Draft.

Approved.

Deprecated.

Retired.

---

## Constraints

Every registered object shall have one
Canonical Identifier.

No registered object may exist outside
the Registry.

Identifiers shall never be reused.

---
