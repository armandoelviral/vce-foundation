# Canonical Term Structure

Version

1.0

Status

Draft

---

## Purpose

Define the normative structure of every
Canonical Commerce Term.

Every term shall share exactly the same
semantic structure.

---

## Canonical Identifier

Every term shall possess one immutable
Canonical Identifier.

Example

CKP-TERM-000001

---

## Preferred Name

Exactly one preferred business name.

---

## Canonical Definition

Exactly one normative definition.

The definition shall describe business
meaning rather than implementation.

---

## Business Meaning

Describe why the concept exists inside
Commerce.

---

## Allowed Synonyms

Alternative business names explicitly
accepted.

---

## Forbidden Synonyms

Ambiguous terms that shall never be used.

---

## Relationships

Semantic relationships with other
Canonical Terms.

---

## Applies To

Commerce domains where the term is valid.

Examples

Retail

Wholesale

Marketplace

Ecommerce

Distribution

---

## Normative Claims

Business statements verified by executable
Specifications.

---

## Business Examples

Representative commerce examples.

---

## References

External standards or internal
specifications.

---

## Status

Draft

Approved

Deprecated

Retired

---

## Constraints

Every Canonical Term shall define every
section.

No section may be omitted.

Canonical structure defined.
