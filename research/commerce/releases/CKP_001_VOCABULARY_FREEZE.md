modified routinely.

## Extension Policy

New Knowledge Objects may extend the frozen
baseline without redefining existing
canonical semantics.

Existing Canonical Identifiers shall never
be reused.

Existing canonical definitions may evolve
only through governed versioning.

Domain-specific vocabularies shall consume
canonical Commerce Knowledge Objects without
private redefinition.
