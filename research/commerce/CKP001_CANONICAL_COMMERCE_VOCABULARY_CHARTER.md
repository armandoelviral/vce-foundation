# CKP-001

Title

Canonical Commerce Vocabulary

Version

1.0

Status

Draft

---

## Vision

Establish the canonical business language for
the Commerce Knowledge Platform.

The Vocabulary shall become the normative
semantic foundation for every commerce
capability implemented by HAS.

---

## Mission

Define a single, verifiable, and canonical
vocabulary describing commerce concepts.

The Vocabulary shall eliminate ambiguity
across Retail, Wholesale, Ecommerce,
Marketplace, Omnichannel, Distribution,
Social Commerce, Franchise, and
Informal Commerce.

---

## Principles

Knowledge precedes implementation.

Vocabulary precedes ontology.

Ontology precedes models.

Models precede services.

Services precede applications.

Canonical terms precede private terminology.

Semantic identity precedes presentation labels.

## Success Criteria

Every commerce capability shall reference
canonical terms.

No capability shall introduce private
terminology.

Every canonical term shall have a stable
identifier.

Every canonical term shall have one preferred
name.

Every canonical term shall have one canonical
definition.

Semantic ambiguity shall be explicitly
identified and resolved.

Retail, Wholesale, Ecommerce, Marketplace,
Omnichannel, Social Commerce, Franchise,
Distribution, and Informal Commerce shall
share the same semantic foundation.

The Vocabulary shall become the single
source of semantic truth for the Commerce
Knowledge Platform.
