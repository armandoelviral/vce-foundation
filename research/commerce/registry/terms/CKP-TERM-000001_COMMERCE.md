# CKP-TERM-000001

## Preferred Name

Commerce

## Canonical Definition

The coordinated exchange of products,
services, value, rights, or obligations
between economic participants through one
or more commercial channels.

## Business Meaning

Commerce is the parent business domain that
contains Retail, Wholesale, Ecommerce,
Marketplace, Social Commerce, Informal
Commerce, and related exchange models.

## Allowed Synonyms

Commercial Activity.

## Forbidden Synonyms

Retail.

Sales.

Trade Operation.

## Relationships

Retail.

Wholesale.

Ecommerce.

Informal Commerce.

Channel.

## Applies To

All Commerce Domains.

## Normative Claims

Commerce shall not be treated as equivalent
to Retail.

Commerce may occur through formal or
informal channels.

## Business Examples

A brand selling directly to a consumer.

A wholesaler supplying independent stores.

An informal seller offering products through
a street market.
