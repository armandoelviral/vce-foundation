# CKP-TERM-000005

## Preferred Name

Informal Commerce

## Canonical Definition

Commercial activity conducted outside fully
formalized organizational, regulatory,
contractual, taxation, or infrastructure
frameworks.

## Business Meaning

Informal Commerce includes legitimate
commercial practices that may operate with
limited formal registration, infrastructure,
documentation, or standardized processes.

## Allowed Synonyms

Informal Trade.

Community Commerce.

## Forbidden Synonyms

Illegal Commerce.

Street Retail.

Unregulated Sales.

## Relationships

Commerce.

Retail.

Wholesale.

Customer.

Channel.

Product.

## Applies To

Street Markets.

Mobile Selling.

Home-Based Selling.

Social Selling.

Community Distribution.

## Normative Claims

Informal Commerce shall not be assumed to be
illegal.

Informal Commerce may contain Retail and
Wholesale transactions.

## Business Examples

A street-market apparel seller.

A home-based reseller using messaging apps.

A mobile vendor serving a local community.
