# CKP-TERM-000003

## Preferred Name

Wholesale

## Canonical Definition

The commercial sale of products or services
to another business, reseller, institution,
or commercial intermediary rather than to
the final consumer.

## Business Meaning

Wholesale supports resale, redistribution,
institutional use, or commercial consumption.

## Allowed Synonyms

Wholesale Commerce.

## Forbidden Synonyms

Retail.

Bulk Sale.

Distribution.

## Relationships

Commerce.

Product.

Inventory.

Channel.

Customer.

## Applies To

B2B Commerce.

Distribution.

Marketplace.

Informal Commerce.

## Normative Claims

Wholesale shall identify a business or
institutional buyer.

Order quantity alone shall not determine
whether a transaction is Wholesale.

## Business Examples

A manufacturer selling cartons to a retailer.

A distributor supplying independent stores.

A wholesaler selling apparel bundles to
market vendors.
