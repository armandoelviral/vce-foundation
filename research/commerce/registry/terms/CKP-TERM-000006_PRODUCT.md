# CKP-TERM-000006

## Preferred Name

Product

## Canonical Definition

A commercial offering that may be identified,
presented, priced, sold, transferred,
distributed, or consumed.

## Business Meaning

Product is the general commercial concept
that may contain one or more sellable
variants or SKUs.

## Allowed Synonyms

Commercial Product.

Offering.

## Forbidden Synonyms

SKU.

Item.

Inventory.

## Relationships

SKU.

Inventory.

Retail.

Wholesale.

Customer.

## Applies To

All Commerce Domains.

## Normative Claims

A Product may contain multiple SKUs.

Product and Inventory shall remain distinct
concepts.

## Business Examples

A shirt style offered in multiple sizes.

A fragrance available in several package
formats.

A service bundle sold through Ecommerce.
