# CKP-TERM-000007

## Preferred Name

SKU

## Canonical Definition

A uniquely managed sellable or stock-tracked
commercial variant within an organization.

## Business Meaning

SKU identifies the operational unit used for
assortment, inventory, ordering, pricing,
allocation, and sales analysis.

## Allowed Synonyms

Stock Keeping Unit.

## Forbidden Synonyms

Product.

UPC.

Item.

## Relationships

Product.

Inventory.

Retail.

Wholesale.

Channel.

## Applies To

Retail.

Wholesale.

Ecommerce.

Marketplace.

Distribution.

Informal Commerce.

## Normative Claims

Every SKU shall belong to one Product.

A SKU identifier is organization-specific
unless explicitly standardized.

## Business Examples

A blue toddler shirt in size 3T.

A 100 ml fragrance package.

A carton configuration sold to wholesalers.
