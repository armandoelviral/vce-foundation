# CKP-TERM-000009

## Preferred Name

Customer

## Canonical Definition

A person, household, business, institution,
or authorized representative that enters or
may enter a commercial relationship.

## Business Meaning

Customer identifies the commercial party
receiving, purchasing, subscribing to, or
contracting for an offering.

## Allowed Synonyms

Buyer.

Client.

## Forbidden Synonyms

Consumer.

Shopper.

User.

## Relationships

Commerce.

Retail.

Wholesale.

Product.

Channel.

## Applies To

All Commerce Domains.

## Normative Claims

A Customer may be an individual or an
organization.

Customer, Shopper, Consumer, and User shall
not be assumed to be equivalent.

## Business Examples

A consumer purchasing apparel.

A retailer purchasing wholesale stock.

A company subscribing to a commerce service.
