# CKP-TERM-000010

## Preferred Name

Channel

## Canonical Definition

A defined commercial path through which
participants discover, negotiate, order,
purchase, deliver, receive, or support a
commercial offering.

## Business Meaning

Channel represents how commercial activity
reaches and interacts with customers or
business partners.

## Allowed Synonyms

Commerce Channel.

Sales Channel.

## Forbidden Synonyms

Store.

Marketplace.

Platform.

## Relationships

Commerce.

Retail.

Wholesale.

Ecommerce.

Informal Commerce.

Customer.

## Applies To

All Commerce Domains.

## Normative Claims

A Channel may be physical, digital, human,
organizational, or hybrid.

Channel and business model shall remain
distinct concepts.

## Business Examples

A physical store.

A brand Ecommerce site.

A marketplace seller account.

A street-market stall.

A messaging-based reseller network.
