# CKP-TERM-000008

## Preferred Name

Inventory

## Canonical Definition

The quantified commercial availability,
ownership, custody, expectation, reservation,
or movement of products or SKUs.

## Business Meaning

Inventory represents stock state across
locations, channels, organizations, and
commercial processes.

## Allowed Synonyms

Stock.

## Forbidden Synonyms

Product.

Assortment.

Merchandise.

## Relationships

SKU.

Product.

Retail.

Wholesale.

Channel.

## Applies To

Retail.

Wholesale.

Ecommerce.

Marketplace.

Distribution.

Informal Commerce.

## Normative Claims

Inventory shall reference a quantity and a
defined stock state.

Inventory shall remain distinct from Product
identity.

## Business Examples

Ten units available in a store.

Reserved Ecommerce stock.

Wholesale stock awaiting allocation.
