# CKP-TERM-000004

## Preferred Name

Ecommerce

## Canonical Definition

Commerce in which product discovery,
transaction initiation, ordering, payment,
or fulfillment coordination occurs through
digital systems.

## Business Meaning

Ecommerce describes a digital commercial
channel and does not determine whether the
transaction is Retail or Wholesale.

## Allowed Synonyms

Electronic Commerce.

Online Commerce.

## Forbidden Synonyms

Marketplace.

Website.

Digital Retail.

## Relationships

Commerce.

Retail.

Wholesale.

Channel.

Customer.

Product.

## Applies To

Direct-to-Consumer.

B2B Commerce.

Marketplace.

Omnichannel.

Social Commerce.

## Normative Claims

Ecommerce may support Retail or Wholesale.

Ecommerce shall not be treated as synonymous
with Marketplace.

## Business Examples

A consumer buying from a brand website.

A retailer ordering stock through a B2B
portal.

A social-commerce transaction completed
through a digital checkout.
