# CKP-TERM-000002

## Preferred Name

Retail

## Canonical Definition

The commercial sale of products or services
to an end customer for personal, household,
or final consumption.

## Business Meaning

Retail describes a business model based on
selling directly to the final customer.

## Allowed Synonyms

Retail Commerce.

## Forbidden Synonyms

Commerce.

Wholesale.

Store.

## Relationships

Commerce.

Customer.

Product.

Channel.

Inventory.

## Applies To

Physical Retail.

Ecommerce.

Marketplace.

Omnichannel.

Social Commerce.

Informal Commerce.

## Normative Claims

Retail shall involve sale to an end customer.

Retail shall not be defined exclusively by
the presence of a physical store.

## Business Examples

A department store sale.

A direct-to-consumer website purchase.

A market stall selling apparel to a shopper.
