Definitions

Program

Complete engineering initiative.

Milestone

A bounded engineering objective.

Deliverable

A coherent engineering capability.

Asset

A concrete engineering artifact.

Executable Contract

An automated verification protecting an Asset.

---

Rule

Every Asset shall have at least one
Executable Contract.
