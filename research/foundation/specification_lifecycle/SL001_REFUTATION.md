# SL-001 Refutation

Title

Repository Specification Lifecycle Refutation

Version

0.1

Status

Research

---

## Purpose

Attempt to invalidate
the assumptions,
ordering,
universality,
and normative boundaries
defined by

SL-001
Repository Specification Lifecycle.

The objective
of this document
is not
to defend SL-001.

The objective
is to determine
where SL-001 fails.

---

## Refutation Principle

Evidence
shall prevail
over attachment.

A lifecycle stage
shall not remain
mandatory
merely because
it appears
architecturally desirable.

A lifecycle stage
shall remain universal
only when its necessity
can be defended
across applicable
normative artifact families.

---

## R-SL001-001

### Hypothesis

Every normative artifact
originates from
a Commercial Problem.

### Counterexample

Repository governance,

constitutional constraints,

security invariants,

or architectural corrections

may originate from:

Observed failure.

Scientific discovery.

Regulatory obligation.

Safety requirement.

Internal inconsistency.

Architectural contradiction.

### Result

REFUTED.

Commercial Problem
cannot be
the universal
first lifecycle stage.

### Required Correction

The lifecycle
requires a more general
origin concept.

Candidate:

Problem or Evidence Trigger.

Commercial problems
remain
a primary trigger,

not the only trigger.

---

## R-SL001-002

### Hypothesis

Research
must precede
every specification.

### Counterexample

A narrow normative correction
may originate
from direct evidence
of contradiction
or implementation failure

without requiring
a separate
research program.

### Result

PARTIALLY REFUTED.

Research
is mandatory
for foundational
architectural abstraction,

but may not require
a distinct research phase
for every normative change.

### Required Correction

Replace universal
Research stage

with:

Investigation.

Research
may be one
form of investigation.

---

## R-SL001-003

### Hypothesis

Freeze
must always precede
Executable Contract.

### Counterexample

Executable contracts
may provide evidence
during specification development.

Prototype contracts
may expose ambiguity
before freeze.

### Result

REFUTED
as an absolute
temporal prohibition.

### Required Correction

Distinguish:

Exploratory Contract

from

Normative Executable Contract.

Exploratory contracts
may exist
before Freeze.

The authoritative
normative contract
shall correspond
to a frozen
specification baseline.

---

## R-SL001-004

### Hypothesis

Executable Contract
must always precede
Reference Implementation.

### Counterexample

Research prototypes
and pre-normative
implementations
may exist
to test hypotheses.

### Result

REFUTED
as a universal
repository prohibition.

### Required Correction

Distinguish:

Experimental Implementation

from

Reference Implementation.

Experimental implementations
may precede
normative contracts.

A Reference Implementation
shall not.

---

## R-SL001-005

### Hypothesis

Commercial Validation
applies to
every normative artifact.

### Counterexample

Repository Constitution,

Architecture Principles,

Specification Lifecycle,

and internal governance
may not have
direct commercial
validation events.

Their value
may instead be observed
through downstream
Domain Runtime behavior.

### Result

REFUTED.

### Required Correction

Commercial Validation
shall apply
where commercial
applicability exists.

A more general
stage is required.

Candidate:

Operational Validation.

Commercial Validation
is one
specialization.

---

## R-SL001-006

### Hypothesis

Every normative artifact
shall eventually possess
an executable contract.

### Counterexample

Some philosophical
or intentionally
non-operational statements
may not be reducible
to meaningful
machine-verifiable assertions.

A textual-presence test
does not necessarily
verify semantic compliance.

### Result

PARTIALLY REFUTED.

### Finding

Machine-checking
the existence
of normative language

is not equivalent
to verifying
its semantics.

### Required Correction

Executable verification
shall be required
only for requirements
that can be
meaningfully operationalized.

Non-executable
normative requirements
shall require
traceable review evidence.

---

## R-SL001-007

### Hypothesis

All normative families
should use
an identical lifecycle.

### Counterexample

Constitutional Principles,

Architecture Principles,

Domain Specifications,

and Runtime Specifications

have different
promotion criteria
and different
validation surfaces.

### Result

PARTIALLY REFUTED.

### Finding

A common
meta-lifecycle
is defensible.

An identical
operational workflow
is not.

### Required Correction

SL-001 shall define
mandatory lifecycle semantics,

while individual
Specification Families
may define
compatible profiles.

---

## R-SL001-008

### Hypothesis

Freeze implies
future backward compatibility.

### Counterexample

A major-version
normative change
may intentionally
break compatibility.

### Result

REFUTED.

### Required Correction

Freeze shall preserve
the semantics
of the frozen baseline.

Future versions
may introduce
breaking changes

only through
explicit versioning
and declared
compatibility impact.

---

## R-SL001-009

### Hypothesis

Platform Learning
necessarily follows
Reference Implementation.

### Counterexample

A specification
may fail
during review,
refutation,
contract construction,
or implementation.

Each failure
can produce
Platform Learning.

### Result

REFUTED
as strictly sequential.

### Finding

Learning
is continuous.

It is not
a terminal stage.

---

## R-SL001-010

### Hypothesis

The lifecycle
is fundamentally
linear.

### Counterexample

Evidence discovered
at any stage
may invalidate
an earlier assumption.

### Result

REFUTED.

### Required Correction

The lifecycle
shall be iterative
and state-transition based.

Backward transitions
shall be permitted
when new evidence
invalidates
an earlier state.

---

## Consolidated Result

SL-001 Version 1.0 Draft

DOES NOT SURVIVE
unchanged.

The central concept
survives:

The repository benefits
from a common
normative lifecycle.

The original
linear lifecycle
does not.

---

## Surviving Core

A normative artifact
requires:

Trigger.

Investigation.

Specification.

Review.

Refutation.

Baseline.

Verification.

Implementation,
where applicable.

Validation,
where applicable.

Evolution.

These concepts
shall not imply
a strictly linear
execution model.

---

## Required Architectural Changes

SL-001 shall distinguish:

Trigger
from Commercial Problem.

Investigation
from Research.

Exploratory Contract
from Normative Contract.

Experimental Implementation
from Reference Implementation.

Operational Validation
from Commercial Validation.

Syntactic Verification
from Semantic Verification.

Common Lifecycle
from Lifecycle Profile.

Baseline Stability
from Future Compatibility.

Continuous Learning
from terminal Platform Learning.

Linear Process
from state-transition lifecycle.

---

## Refutation Outcome

Status

REFUTED IN PART.

Disposition

REVISE.

Freeze

PROHIBITED
until the identified
issues are resolved.
