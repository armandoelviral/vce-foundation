# Constitutional Principle

Identifier

CP-002

Title

Trust Model First

Version

0.1

Status

Draft

---

## Purpose

Define the constitutional
candidate principle
that architectural
and implementation decisions
shall derive
from an explicit
model of trust,

rather than
allowing implementation
choices to define
the model.

---

## Principle

The Trust Model
shall define
the architecture.

Architecture
shall define
normative specifications.

Normative specifications
shall define
executable contracts.

Executable contracts
shall constrain
implementations.

Implementations
shall realize
the model.

The direction
shall never
be reversed.

---

## Authority Direction

The canonical
direction of authority
is:

Trust Model

↓

Architecture

↓

Normative Specification

↓

Executable Contract

↓

Reference Implementation

↓

Runtime Artifact

↓

Execution

No lower layer
shall redefine
a higher layer.

---

## Implementation Constraint

Implementation convenience
shall never become
normative authority.

Existing code
shall not become
a specification
merely because
it already exists.

A successful implementation
shall not establish
architectural correctness
by itself.

Reference implementations
shall remain
replaceable.

---

## Evidence Over Attachment

Architectural decisions
shall follow
evidence
over attachment.

Historical investment
shall not protect
an abstraction
from refutation.

Implementation familiarity
shall not protect
an abstraction
from refutation.

Architectural elegance
shall not protect
an abstraction
from refutation.

If evidence demonstrates
a better model,

the Platform
shall evolve.

---

## Scientific Discipline

Architectural ideas
shall be treated
as hypotheses
until sufficiently
validated.

A foundational abstraction
shall undergo:

Observation.

Formalization.

Refutation.

Experimental validation.

Cross-domain validation.

Reproducibility analysis.

Architectural promotion.

Only sufficiently
validated abstractions
may be considered
for constitutional
promotion.

---

## Commercial Reality

The Trust Model
shall not emerge
from technology-driven
speculation alone.

Commercial problems
shall provide
real-world questions
for applied
scientific investigation.

Research
shall discover
reusable principles.

Architecture
shall stabilize
those principles.

Domain Runtimes
shall convert
those principles
into commercial value.

---

## Research Boundary

The Trust Model
is presently
under active
foundational research.

Candidate abstractions
shall not be
prematurely frozen.

The current investigation
shall remain open
to the possibility
that Trust
is not
the most fundamental
abstraction.

Evidence may demonstrate
that a more general
appraisal model
better explains
the architecture.

Such a result
shall be accepted
if supported
by stronger evidence.

---

## Candidate Invariants

Model
precedes architecture.

Architecture
precedes implementation.

Evidence
precedes promotion.

Commercial reality
precedes speculative
abstraction.

Implementation
shall not define
normative truth.

The Platform
shall change
its ideas
before forcing
evidence
to fit them.

---

# End of Principle
