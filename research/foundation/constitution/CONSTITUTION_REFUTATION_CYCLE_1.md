# C-R1-001

## Target Claim

The Constitution
defines immutable
constitutional principles.

---

## Challenge

An immutable principle
cannot be corrected
when future evidence
demonstrates
that the principle
is incomplete,
incorrect,
unsafe,
or overgeneralized.

This conflicts
with the repository
discipline of:

Evidence
over attachment.

Refutation
before promotion.

Explicit version evolution.

---

## Counterexample

A constitutional principle
is promoted
based upon
three validated domains.

A fourth independent
domain later demonstrates
that the principle
is false
outside its original scope.

Literal immutability
would prevent correction.

---

## Result

REFUTED.

---

## Finding

Constitutional semantics
may be strongly stable

without being
literally immutable.

A constitutional baseline
shall remain historically
immutable once published.

Future constitutional
versions
shall remain possible.

---
