## Purpose

Evaluate every directed
non-self pair

among:

RC-001.

SL-001.

APC-001.

NAM-001.

ALM-001.

The objective
is to determine

whether each pair
demonstrates:

Normative authority.

Non-authority relationship.

No demonstrated relationship.

Insufficient evidence.

Conflicting evidence.

or a relationship
requiring further
refutation.

No authority edge
shall be admitted
by this document.

---

## Pair Evaluation Method

For every directed pair:

Source.

Target.

Inspect the
canonical source artifact.

Inspect the
canonical target artifact.

Inspect applicable:

Promotion Gate.

Freeze.

Executable Contract.

Authority Scope.

Boundary declarations.

Authority-of-Authority
language.

Record only
evidence explicitly
present
in current authoritative
or preserved
promotion artifacts.

Historical research
may explain provenance

but shall not
create current authority.
