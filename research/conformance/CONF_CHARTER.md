# HAS Conformance Platform Charter

Version

1.0

Status

Draft

---

## Mission

Demonstrate, through executable evidence,
that every traced Normative Claim is
satisfied by the HAS Runtime.

---

## Scope

The Conformance Platform verifies
implementation.

It does not define specifications.

It does not implement runtime behavior.

---

## Responsibilities

Verify Claim Coverage.

Verify Capability Coverage.

Verify Runtime Evidence.

Produce executable Conformance Reports.

---

## Inputs

Specification Platform.

Traceability Registry.

Executable Contracts.

Runtime Test Suite.

---

## Outputs

Conformance Report.

Coverage Metrics.

Evidence Registry.

---

## Success Criteria

Every traced Claim possesses:

- Capability

- Executable Contract

- Executable Evidence

The platform shall compute
overall Conformance.
