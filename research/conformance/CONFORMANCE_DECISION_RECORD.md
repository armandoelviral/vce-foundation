# HAS Conformance Decision Record

Version

1.0

Status

Draft

---

## Purpose

Define the normative output produced by
the Conformance Evaluator.

The Decision Record represents the
objective result of Conformance.

---

## Required Fields

Normative Claim.

Capability.

Executable Contract.

Coverage Status.

Decision.

Failure Reason.

---

## Decision Values

Conformant.

Non-Conformant.

---

## Constraints

Every Decision Record shall reference
exactly one Normative Claim.

Every Decision Record shall reference
exactly one Capability.

Every Decision Record shall reference
exactly one Decision.

---

## Output

Decision Record.
