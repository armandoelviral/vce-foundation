# HAS Claim Coverage Model

Version

1.0

Status

Draft

---

## Purpose

Define how Claim Coverage is computed.

Claim Coverage verifies that every traced
Normative Claim is mapped to at least one
registered Capability.

---

## Coverage Rule

Normative Claim

↓

Capability

↓

Covered

---

## Covered

A Claim is Covered when at least one
registered Capability references it.

---

## Not Covered

A Claim is Not Covered if no Capability
references the Claim.

---

## Output

Claim Coverage Percentage.

Covered Claims.

Missing Claims.
