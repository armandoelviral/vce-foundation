# HAS Conformance Decision Model

Version

1.0

Status

Draft

---

## Purpose

Define how Conformance Decisions are
derived from validated inputs.

The Decision Model specifies the
normative outcomes of the Conformance
Platform.

---

## Decision Inputs

Normative Claim

Capability

Executable Contract

Coverage Status

---

## Decision Rules

If every required input is valid and the
Coverage Status is Covered:

↓

Decision

Conformant

Otherwise:

↓

Decision

Non-Conformant

---

## Failure Reasons

Missing Claim.

Missing Capability.

Missing Contract.

Not Covered.

Undefined Input.

---

## Outputs

Conformance Decision.

Failure Reason.
