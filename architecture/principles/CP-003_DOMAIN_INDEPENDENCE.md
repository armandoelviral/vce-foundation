# Constitutional Principle

Identifier

CP-003

Title

Domain Independence

Version

0.1

Status

Draft

---

## Purpose

Define the constitutional
candidate requirement
that reusable
Platform trust semantics
remain independent
from any individual
commercial,
technical,
scientific,
or regulatory
domain.

---

## Principle

The Platform
shall separate

common trust semantics

from

domain semantics.

The Common Trust Architecture
shall not know
the business meaning
of a Domain Runtime.

Only Domain Specifications
may define
domain semantics.

---

## Domain Agnosticism

The Platform
shall remain
domain-independent
and,
where demonstrated
possible,
domain-agnostic.

Trust infrastructure
shall not require
knowledge of:

Retail.

Finance.

Healthcare.

Legal systems.

Manufacturing.

Scientific Computing.

Artificial Intelligence.

Computer Vision.

Supply Chain.

Robotics.

Or future
application domains.

These are domains.

Trust is not.

---

## Domain Runtime Boundary

Every Domain Runtime
shall define
the semantics required
for its own
commercial problem.

Examples include:

Visual composition
semantics.

Financial risk
semantics.

Clinical evidence
semantics.

Legal authority
semantics.

Domain semantics
shall not leak
into reusable
Platform layers.

---

## Decision Boundary

Reusable Platform layers
shall not issue
domain decisions.

A reusable appraisal
or trust layer
may produce
a domain-neutral
assessment.

Domain policy
shall determine
the corresponding
domain action.

Therefore:

Assessment

is not

Business Decision.

Evidence support

is not

Clinical Authority.

Integrity

is not

Financial Authorization.

Attestation

is not

Legal Consent.

---

## Cross-Domain Validation

A candidate
foundational abstraction
shall not be
declared domain-independent
merely because
it works
in one domain.

Cross-domain independence
shall be demonstrated.

The candidate
shall survive
multiple unrelated
domain contexts
before promotion.

---

## Commercial Narrative Boundary

Every Domain Runtime
shall have
its own
commercial message.

Visual users
shall receive
a visual value
proposition.

Financial users
shall receive
a financial value
proposition.

Clinical users
shall receive
a clinical value
proposition.

Legal users
shall receive
a legal value
proposition.

The Platform
shall not force
one abstract
architectural narrative
upon all customers.

---

## Platform Learning

Domain Runtimes
shall remain
independent,

but they may
contribute validated
architectural knowledge
back to
the Platform.

Only knowledge
that survives
cross-domain
refutation
may become
a reusable
Platform abstraction.

---

## Candidate Invariants

Domain semantics
remain local.

Reusable trust semantics
remain domain-independent.

Domain decisions
remain outside
the shared
architectural core.

No single domain
shall define
the Platform.

Cross-domain evidence
shall govern
generalization.

Commercial narratives
shall remain
domain-specific.

---

# End of Principle
